Mercury retrograde happens three to four times a year. The internet treats it like a curse. Vedic timing treats it like a *review cycle*.

## What actually shifts

Mercury rules communication, contracts, travel logistics, and short-term decisions. Retrograde periods favor:

- Reopening unfinished conversations
- Editing plans instead of launching brand-new ones
- Catching errors you would have missed at full speed

They are weaker windows for rushed signatures, impulsive purchases, or assuming everyone heard you the first time.

## A calmer way to use it

1. **List open loops** — messages, deals, and trips that need a second look.
2. **Delay non-urgent launches** if your chart shows Mercury stress on the topic.
3. **Double-check details** — dates, names, payment links, meeting links.

## Ask Peak during retrograde

Peak can weigh Mercury's condition against *your* dasha and the houses tied to your question — career news, a family talk, or a money decision — so you get timing advice instead of vague cosmic weather.
