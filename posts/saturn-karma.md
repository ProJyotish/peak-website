Saturn gets a bad reputation. In practice, Saturn is the part of the chart that asks: *will this still matter in five years?*

## What Saturn tends to do

* Slows timelines so foundations can set
* Rewards consistency over intensity
* Exposes shortcuts that looked clever in the short term

When Saturn is active for career, health, or responsibility themes, progress often feels heavier - and more durable.

## Karma, without the mystique

"Karma" here is mostly feedback: the quality of your systems, habits, and commitments shows up in outcomes. Saturn periods are when that feedback is hardest to ignore.

## How to work with it

* Prefer sustainable routines over heroics
* Finish what you started before stacking new goals
* Treat delays as information, not rejection
