The decision is whether this period supports leaving a salary to build independent income, or whether the chart still wants a runway.

## What the chart is actually answering

Peak weighs the **10th** (status, employment) against the **11th and 3rd** (gains, self-directed effort). The tell is runway versus overreach: a dasha that can carry invoices and clients is different from a dasha that only inflates the fantasy of quitting.

This is not the same page as changing employers. You are asking about self-started income, not a new boss.

## What Peak does next

Set a Peak goal with a target quit date. Ask Peak to read the months up to that date.

[Ask Peak about quitting to start](https://wa.me/919560057789?text=I%20want%20to%20quit%20to%20start%20something%20by%20%5Bdate%5D.)

Keep savings months, dependents, and a first-revenue plan on the table. Timing without a runway is still a gamble.
