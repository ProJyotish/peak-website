The decision is which months favor interviews and offers after time away — not whether you are “allowed” to return.

## What the chart is actually answering

Peak reads the **10th** (profession) together with the **1st** (vitality, presentation) after a gap. A return window is a recovery of both capacity and visibility. That is a different lever from changing jobs while already employed.

Name the kind of work you want back, and a date you are aiming at.
