The decision is whether to accept **this** offer now, or wait for a later window. Name both paths when you ask.

## What the chart is actually answering

This is a **two-path compare**: the current 10th-house situation versus the start date of the offer. Peak is not re-answering “should I change jobs.” It is asking whether the months after joining are a constructive start, or a mis-timed leap.

Bring the joining date if you have one. Timing of the first 90 days matters as much as the title on the letter.
