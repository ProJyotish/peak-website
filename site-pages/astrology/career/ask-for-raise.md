The decision is whether to push compensation **this cycle**, or wait until a better conversation window.

## What the chart is actually answering

Raises live in short **transit and hora windows** more than in a multi-year dasha. Peak looks at visibility versus confrontation: whether the next weeks support being seen, or whether the same ask lands as conflict.

This is not a job-change page. You are staying, and asking for the number to move.
