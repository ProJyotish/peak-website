The decision is which months favor a real training block (race, return to gym, ambitious load) versus months that raise injury risk.

> Not medical clearance. Get that from a clinician before you load the body.

## What the chart is actually answering

Peak reads **1st/6th Mars windows**: vitality and effort versus inflammation and overreach. Different from burnout (drain) and from post-baby recovery (1st after a 5th/8th cycle).
