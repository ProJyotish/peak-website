The decision is when to schedule a **planned** procedure and recovery — not whether you should have surgery.

> This is timing support, not medical advice. Your surgeon’s urgency always wins.

## What the chart is actually answering

Peak uses the **8th** (intervention, recovery) with the **6th** (illness, repair). Caregiver cases (a parent’s date) use the same levers on that person’s chart, not yours alone.
