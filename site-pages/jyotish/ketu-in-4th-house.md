**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 4th house (Chaturtha Bhava, the house of home and mother) unsettles domestic peace, denies happiness from the mother, causes the loss of paternal property through one's own associates, and prevents the native from living long in one home. No reversal is offered in the source verse.

## Ketu in the 4th House: Mind and Domestic Peace
The controlling verse is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Ketu, Verse 4). The fourth house, *Chaturtha Bhava* (also *Bandhu Bhava*), governs the mother (*Matru*), domestic happiness (*Sukha*), and, per the *Bhavartha Ratnakara*'s table of contents, fortune, luxuries, and children. The pivot term is *Vyagrata*: agitated, anxious, distracted. Since the fourth house is the house of *Sukha* itself, Ketu sitting there comes close to contradicting the house's purpose directly, leaving an undercurrent of emotional instability running beneath daily life.

## Ketu in the 4th House: Mother
The verse is unambiguous: *Maatuh sukheno kadaachit*, the native "never receives happiness from the mother." This points to separation, an absence of maternal warmth, or early distress centered on the mother. The text adds that the native's own associates and social circle (*Suhadvargatah*) are cast unfavorably too, functioning as one of the mechanisms that erodes the domestic foundation rather than protecting it.

## Ketu in the 4th House: Property and Home
Two distinct losses are named. *Paitrakam nashayeti*: the native loses or destroys paternal, ancestral property, attributed specifically to the influence of associates and kinsmen rather than the native acting alone. And *Chiram no vaset svegrihe*: the native "does not live long in their own house," frequent relocation or rented housing rather than a fixed address.

## Ketu in the 4th House: Health Effects
The *Brihat Jataka* maps the fourth sign to the *Hridaya*, the heart (Chapter 1, Stanza 4). Combined with general *Sharire vyatha* (bodily pain) language elsewhere in the *Chamatkara Chintaamani*, this points toward cardiac or chest-area vulnerability, though this specific combination is a synthesis across sources rather than a claim Verse 4 makes on its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does Ketu in the 4th house affect the relationship with the mother?** Yes, the verse states directly that the native never receives happiness from the mother.
**Does Ketu in the 4th house cause loss of property?** Yes, specifically paternal or ancestral property, attributed to the influence of associates.
**Is Ketu in the 4th house linked to heart problems?** The link is a reasonable scholarly synthesis (heart anatomy plus general Ketu affliction language), not a single verse stating it outright.
