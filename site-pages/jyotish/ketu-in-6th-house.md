**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 6th house (Satru Bhava, the house of enemies and disease) is one of the genuinely favorable Ketu placements: it destroys disease and eliminates enemies outright, but it trades this victory for modest wealth, loss connected to livestock, and humiliation for maternal uncles.

## Ketu in the 6th House: Health Effects
The *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Ketu, Verse 6) supplies the governing verse: *"तमः षष्ठभागेगते षष्ठभावे भवेत् मातुलान् मनभण्गो रिपूणाम् । विनाशश्चतुष्पात् सुखं तुच्छवित्तं शरीरं सदानामयं व्याधिनाशः॥"* Ketu here goes by *Tamah* (shadow) and *Sikhin* (crested one). The classical logic is a specific paradox: a malefic placed in a *Dusthana* (difficult house) can destroy that house's own negative output rather than compound it. The sixth house generates illness by nature, and the verse's term *Vyadhi-nasha*, destruction of disease, produces *Sada-anamayam*: a state perpetually free of sickness. Ketu's shadow obscures disease before it can take hold, leaving the native with real resistance to chronic infirmity.

## Ketu in the 6th House: Enemies Destroyed
The same logic applies to opposition. *Ripunam-vinasha*, destruction of enemies, is described in absolute terms: the native decisively eliminates rivals rather than merely managing them, arriving close to an "enemy-less" state through Ketu's unpredictable, shadow-driven force.

## Ketu in the 6th House: What It Costs
None of this comes free. *Tuchha-vitta*: wealth stays trifling and meager, even as the native wins the fights and stays healthy. *Vinashas-chatushpat* adds loss connected to four-legged animals, traditionally livestock. And *Mula-manabhanga* extends the cost to the maternal side of the family: the native's maternal uncles face humiliation or a loss of standing.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Ketu in the 6th house a good placement?** Yes, for health and conflict specifically: the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* frames it as one of the placements where a malefic in a difficult house destroys that house's own negative output.
**Does Ketu in the 6th house bring wealth?** No, the text specifically names wealth as *Tuchha-vitta*, trifling, even while health and victory over enemies are strong.
**Does Ketu in the 6th house affect maternal relatives?** Yes, *Mula-manabhanga* names humiliation or loss of standing specifically for maternal uncles.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Ketu, Verse 6. *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1: sixth house as the belly and navel of the *Kalapurusha*, and the house of enemies. *Phaladeepika*, Chapter 16: general significations of the sixth house, including diseases, debts, and maternal relatives. *Brihat Samhita*, Chapter 11: Ketu as *Sikhin*, contextual only.
