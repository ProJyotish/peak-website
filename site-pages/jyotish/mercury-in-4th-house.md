*Analysis based on traditional Jyotish texts. Full citations at the end of this page.*

**Quick answer:** Mercury in the 4th house (Caturthe Bhava, the house of home, mother, and happiness) gives a charming, learned personality, professional distinction as a writer, administrator, or landholder that is built without paternal inheritance, and a warm, conversation-driven bond with the mother. Health concerns in this placement trend toward the heart and track with mental strain rather than physical weakness.

## Mercury in the 4th House: Personality and Learning
*Chamatkara Chintamani* (Chapter 4, Budha Verse 4) calls the native *charu mitra*, a genuinely charming friend, the kind of person who draws good company and builds a home full of pleasant social contact. Because Mercury governs *buddhi*, intelligence, its presence in this house of education produces a native who is inherently learned, with *Sukha*, happiness, arriving through the acquisition of knowledge rather than comfort for its own sake. The *Kendra* placement, an angular house, keeps that mental disposition peaceful and well-ordered, provided the planet is not heavily afflicted (*Phaladeepika*, Chapter 1).

## Mercury in the 4th House: Career and Writing
The verse credits the native with real delegated authority: *vishesadhikrid*, a special officer holding specific authority, and *bhuminatho ganasya*, someone who leads a group or administers land. Beyond *lekhaka*, a writer who executes others' orders in writing, the verse uses *likhyate*: the native becomes someone written about, known through their own written work. **The pen builds the reputation, not the inheritance.** That's the catch the text names directly: *no dhanam ca paitrikam*, no inherited paternal wealth, and *tadashaparah*, meaning that despite real authority, the native often operates within someone else's larger vision rather than setting one.

## Mercury in the 4th House: Home, Mother, and Property
As the house of *Matru*, the native's relationship with the mother and relatives runs through conversation and intellectual exchange rather than pure sentiment, consistent with the *charu mitra* warmth already described. Vehicles tend toward the modern and frequently updated, in keeping with Mercury's youthful character, and success in land and property follows from the *bhuminatho* signification (*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Budha Verse 4).

## Mercury in the 4th House: Health
In the *Kalapurusha* mapping, the 4th house corresponds to the *Hridaya*, the heart (*Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verse 3). Because Mercury here is "learned" rather than physically robust by nature, health issues in this region tend to track with mental strain and high intellectual load rather than pure physical vulnerability (*Phaladeepika*, Chapter 1).

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does Mercury in the 4th house bring inherited wealth?** No, the text names the opposite directly: whatever success comes, comes without paternal inheritance.
**Does Mercury in the 4th house affect the relationship with the mother?** Yes, the bond runs through conversation and intellectual exchange rather than pure sentiment.
**Is Mercury in the 4th house good for career?** Yes, it favours writing, administration, and land, though authority here is often exercised on someone else's behalf.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Chapter 4, Budha Verse 4 (career, writing, inherited wealth); *Phaladeepika*, Chapter 8 (learning, happiness, conveyances) and Chapter 1 (Kendra strength); *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verse 3 (Matru, Hridaya, Kendra manifestation).
