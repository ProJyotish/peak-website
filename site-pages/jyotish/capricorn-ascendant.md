**Quick answer:** The Capricorn ascendant is ruled by Saturn and shaped like a crocodile with a deer's face, governing the two knees in the *Kalapurusha*. The native combines predatory tenacity with constant vigilance, achieving through slow, deliberate effort rather than shortcuts, at the cost of an argumentative streak and real anxiety about family and associates.

## Fundamental Identity: Saturn, the Knees, and the Crocodile with a Deer's Face

Makara (Capricorn), the tenth sign, signifies the two knees (*Janudvayam*) of the *Kalapurusha*, is a moveable sign (*Chara Rasi*), and takes the shape of a crocodile with the face of a deer (*Makara-mrigasya-vaktra*), ruled by Saturn (*Sauri*) and carrying the older synonym *Akokera* (*Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verses 3, 4, 8).

## Body and Bearing

The knees carry real weight in the physical picture, literally and structurally. What sits in the lagna sharpens the rest: Saturn present brings *Sthula-drishti*, thick or squint-set vision, with afflictions the texts trace to the native's own persistent actions (*Swakrutyavyadhibadhah*), not chance. The Moon, unless placed in Taurus, produces a thin-bodied constitution (*Kshina-deha*), weakened by the sign's cold, restrictive nature. The Sun grants a tall, pole-like frame (*Tunga-yashti*). Other placements: Mercury gives a gold-like body and skill in medicine (*Chikitsavido*); Jupiter gives a divine body with low vitality (*Alpa-virya*); Venus gives beautiful limbs and a pull toward multiple partners; Rahu gives eloquence at the cost of steadiness in marriage; Ketu brings physical pain and wind-related disease.

## Temperament

Saturn's own disposition, when it sits in the lagna, is not flattering: argumentative (*Vivadi*), sharply acquisitive (*Ati-trishno*), prone to envy (*Matsara*), quick to anger. The Moon's version runs differently but no more comfortably: dull (*Jada*), hard of hearing (*Shrotra-hina*). Underneath either placement sits a steady undercurrent of anxiety about family, heirs, and close associates.

## Behavioral Tendencies and Life Path

Vitality and efficacy run stronger at night (*Ratri-sangyaka*), and the feet-first rising (*Prishthodaya*) sets the tempo for everything: achievement through laborious, step-by-step effort. Spiritually, if Saturn, Mercury, and Mars are conjoined in Capricorn with Rahu in Pisces, the native enjoys dips in holy rivers such as the Ganga (*Bhavartha Ratnakara*, Capricorn Section, Verse 11).

## Special Yogas for Capricorn Ascendant (Bhavartha Ratnakara)

A major *Rajayoga* forms if Mars and Venus are in the lagna while Jupiter sits in Capricorn: the *dasas* of Mercury and Jupiter prove exceptionally fortunate and status-enhancing (Verse 10).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the personality traits of a Capricorn ascendant?**
A Capricorn ascendant is disciplined and persistent, ruled by Saturn, combining predatory tenacity with constant vigilance and an argumentative streak.

**What happens when the Moon is placed in a Capricorn ascendant?**
Unless the Moon is in Taurus, it produces a thin-bodied constitution here, since it is weakened by the sign's cold, restrictive nature.

**What is the rajayoga for a Capricorn ascendant?**
Mars and Venus placed in the lagna, with Jupiter in Capricorn, forms a major rajayoga that makes both the Mercury and Jupiter dashas exceptionally fortunate.
