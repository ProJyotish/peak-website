**Quick answer:** Sun in the 4th house (Caturtha Bhava, the heart of the Kalapurusha) gives genuine public splendor and official standing, paired with conflict or separation from kinsmen, a tendency to live away from the birthplace, a specific risk of losing honor in open confrontation, and a restlessness that never fully settles. This is a placement that photographs well in public and does not rest easy at home.

## What Sun in the 4th House Means for Status and Home
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Surya Phalam, Verse 4), read against the *Brihat Jataka*'s definition of the fourth house as *Hridaya*, the heart, of the *Kalapurusha* (Chapter 1, Stanza 3). *Atishobhadhikari*: the native holds a "portfolio," official dignity, genuine splendor and standing. The suffix *-adhikari* matters here: it denotes someone who holds actual office or formal responsibility, not just a reputation for it.

The mechanism behind everything else in the verse traces back to this house's own nature. The *Chamatkara Chintamani*'s own Verse 1 establishes the Sun as carrying *Pitta*, fire, and *santapa*, distress, by nature. Heat in the heart's house burns the very quality the fourth house is supposed to provide: emotional security, rootedness, home. What follows in the verse is that same fire finding different outlets.

## Sun in the 4th House: Family and Domestic Life
*Vigrahe bandhutopi*: conflict or outright separation from one's own kinsmen. Whatever splendor this native carries in public, the same verse deducts from the domestic side of the ledger. Support that should come easily from family is instead where the friction concentrates.

## Sun in the 4th House: Foreign Residence and Conflict
*Pravasi*: a tendency to live away from the birthplace, in foreign lands, the fourth house's own roots unsettled by the Sun's heat rather than steadied by it. *Vipakshahave manabhangam* adds a specific vulnerability: loss of honor in open combat with enemies, not quiet rivalry but direct confrontation. **The native's public standing and their domestic and combative reality point in opposite directions, and the text doesn't try to reconcile them.**

## Sun in the 4th House: Mental Peace
*Kadachinna shantam bhavet tasya chetah*: the native's consciousness (*cetah*, the deeper seat of feeling, distinct from the ordinary mind) never finds peace. This is the throughline for the whole verse. Splendor, kin conflict, foreign residence, restless consciousness are not four separate misfortunes; they are the same solar fire, established as *Pitta* and *santapa* in the very first verse of this text, finding a different outlet in each area of life.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Sun in the 4th house good or bad for career?** It's genuinely good for status and official standing (*Atishobhadhikari*), but that public strength comes with real domestic cost.
**Does Sun in the 4th house cause problems at home?** Yes, the text names conflict or separation from kinsmen directly (*vigrahe bandhutopi*).
**Does Sun in the 4th house indicate living abroad?** Yes, *pravasi* points specifically to residence away from the birthplace, in foreign lands.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Surya Phalam, Verse 4 (splendor and authority, kin conflict, foreign residence, loss of honor in conflict, restless consciousness), and Surya Phalam, Verse 1 (the Sun's *Pitta* and *santapa* nature, cited for the causal link to the heart). *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Stanza 3 (fourth house as *Hridaya*).
