**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 8th house (Randhra Bhava, the house of transformation) brings rectal and pelvic health trouble and a freeze on liquid wealth, unless Ketu occupies one of four specific signs, in which case the same house turns into a source of *Artha-Labha*, wealth gain.

## Ketu in the 8th House: Health Effects
The governing source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Chapter on Ketu, Verse 8). The eighth house carries the name *Randhra*, an aperture or point of vulnerability, alongside its standard significations of death and longevity. Ketu here, *Shikhi*, also *Rahuccha*, brings its usual obstructive tendency to the native's vitality. The verse is specific: *guḍā pīḍyate*, rectal pain and disease, and *arśārogair*, hemorrhoids. This lines up with the *Brihat Jataka*'s mapping of the eighth house to the genital organ of the *Kalapurusha* (Chapter 1, Verse 3), confirming the pelvic and excretory region as the placement's specific zone of vulnerability.

## Ketu in the 8th House: Money and Momentum
Two distinct obstructions are named. *Svadravyasyarodhaḥ*, stoppage of one's own wealth: capital and resources that get frozen rather than lost outright, a liquidity problem more than a poverty sentence. And *Bhayaṃ vāhanādeḥ*, fear connected to vehicles and conveyances, a specific dread around transport.

## Ketu in the 8th House in Scorpio (Vrishchika), Virgo (Kanya), Aries (Mesha), and Gemini (Mithuna)
Verse 8 names four signs in which the entire verdict inverts from obstruction to *Artha-Labha*, gain of wealth. In Scorpio, Ketu is in compatible territory and sheds its maleficence toward resources outright. In Virgo, the standard health warnings are mitigated and the house's transformative energy converts into material prosperity. In Aries, the wealth-stoppage rule is overridden in favor of accumulation. In Gemini, the obstruction dissolves into gain. Outside these four signs, the difficult reading stands.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Ketu in the 8th house always bad for health?** The verse names rectal and hemorrhoidal trouble as the standard result, without a stated health-specific exception outside of Virgo's general mitigation.
**Which signs reverse Ketu in the 8th house into wealth gain?** Scorpio, Virgo, Aries, and Gemini, per the *Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Verse 8.
**Does Ketu in the 8th house cause poverty?** Not poverty outright; the text names *Svadravyasyarodhaḥ*, a stoppage or freezing of one's own wealth, rather than its destruction.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Chapter on Ketu, Verse 8. *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verse 3: eighth house mapped to the genital organ of the *Kalapurusha*.
