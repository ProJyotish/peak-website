**Quick answer:** Mars in the 10th house (Dasham Bhava, called *Meshoorana*) is one of the genuinely strong placements for Mangal. The texts describe self-made social rise regardless of birth circumstances, a commanding, lion-like presence in the native's career, and prosperity that extends to benefit the whole family. When Mars sits in Capricorn, its sign of exaltation, alongside Mercury and Saturn, a specific spiritual merit is named as well.

## What Mars in the 10th House Means for Career and Status
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Verse 10). The verse is unambiguous: even a native born into *Varako'pi*, wretched or humble circumstances, is destined for *Rajatulya*, status equal to a king. This is named as a catalyst for real, radical social mobility, not a gradual improvement. The text adds that these achievements are *Svatah Siddha*, self-proven, arising from the native's own merit rather than favor granted by someone else.

## Mars in the 10th House: Personality and Leadership
The verse asks, *Kanthiravah kim dvitiyah*, is he a second lion, applying the same lion image used elsewhere in the Mangal material, here to a native ruling his professional domain specifically. The tenth house's link to the knees of the *Kalapurusha* (*Brihat Jataka*, 1.4, distinguishing it from the ninth house's thighs) gives a literal reading too: this is the placement providing the structural joints to stand and carry the weight of authority. Mars, generally classified as *Krura* (cruel) by the *Brihat Jataka* and *Brihat Samhita*, is ennobled here. The heat doesn't disappear, it converts into command.

## Mars in the 10th House: Effects on Family and Lineage
The *Chamatkara Chintamani* asks, *Kule tasya kim mangalam*, what auspiciousness could possibly be missing from the clan of a native like this? It's a rhetorical question with an obvious answer: none. This is a rare instance in the Mangal material of the planet's force being fully absorbed by the people around the native, rather than spent at their expense.

## Mars in the 10th House in Capricorn (Makara)
The *Bhavartha Ratnakara* (10.11) records a sign-specific case: if Mars sits in Capricorn, its sign of exaltation, together with Mercury and Saturn, the native earns the merit of bathing in the sacred Ganga, and the text ties this specifically to the major periods, the Dasas, of Mars itself. This is one of the few places in the Mangal material where a house result, a sign condition, and a Dasa window are all named together in the same breath.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 10th house good or bad for career?** Strongly good. This is named as one of the more reliable placements for self-made rise in status and career.
**Does Mars in the 10th house benefit the family?** Yes, the text asks rhetorically what could possibly be missing from the clan of such a native, treating his rise as the family's rise.
**What happens when Mars is exalted in the 10th house in Capricorn?** With Mercury and Saturn also present, the native earns the merit of bathing in the Ganga during Mars Dasa.

## Sources
