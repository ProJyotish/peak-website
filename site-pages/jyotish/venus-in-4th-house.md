**Quick answer:** Venus in the 4th house (Chaturtha Bhava, the house of home and inner comfort) gives a status so secure it does not depend on public opinion, a mother who provides sustained nourishment and support, and a life filled with recognition and tribute rather than material rewards that have to be fought for. The classical verse is almost entirely unqualified about this placement.

## Venus in the 4th House: Standing Above Public Opinion
The source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* of Bhattanarayana, Verse 4, for Venus in the *Chaturtha Bhava*. The *Brihat Jataka* maps this house to the **Heart** (*Hridaya*) of the *Kalapurusha* and classifies it as *Sukha*, happiness and comfort (*Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Rasi table), and that is the frame for what the verse says next. *Mahitve-adhiko*: the native attains a status of such inherent greatness that the shifting opinions of the crowd become irrelevant. The verse poses this as a rhetorical question, asking who among the people, angry or pleased, could actually move the native's position (*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Chaturtha Bhava, Verse 4). The native's standing is settled before the opinions even arrive.

## Venus in the 4th House: Mother and Home
*Sanjananya*, the mother, provides sustained nourishment and support from birth (*Janmatah*). The verse's term *Adhinapi* carries a specific nuance: even in a state of dependence or subordination, the native remains continuously sustained and elevated rather than diminished by it (*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Chaturtha Bhava, Verse 4).

## Venus in the 4th House: Wealth That Arrives, Not Earned
Here is the distinguishing feature of this house's version of Venus. *Upayanaireva Purnah*: the native is filled with tributes and offerings (*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Chaturtha Bhava, Verse 4). This is a different mode of prosperity than Venus produces elsewhere. In the second house, Venus rewards self-presentation, fine speech and fine clothes. In the third, it works against the house's native valor, producing a timid commander. The fourth house is neither. It is a receptive house, and here Venus does not need to fight for status or dress for it. Recognition and reward simply arrive.

The source is candid about its own limits: this verse says nothing about health, specific professional titles, or sibling longevity for this placement, and the honest position is to say so rather than fill the gap with generic astrology.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Venus in the 4th house good for home and property?** Yes, this is one of the least complicated placements in the study, with status, maternal support, and prosperity all described without qualification.
**Does Venus in the 4th house favor the mother?** Yes, the text specifically names sustained maternal nourishment and support as a core result.
**Does Venus in the 4th house bring wealth through effort?** No, the distinguishing feature here is that recognition and reward arrive through tribute rather than active labor.

## Sources
