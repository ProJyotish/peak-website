**Quick answer:** Mars in the 11th house (Labha Bhava, the house of gains) builds real, tangible wealth through livestock and vehicles, alongside a bold, confrontational temperament that can alienate people even as it commands respect. The same placement brings friction to children and dominance over enemies through planetary aspect, and names one specific habit, malicious slander, capable of erasing the wealth suddenly.

## What Mars in the 11th House Means for Personality and Temperament
The source is a single detailed verse in the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Mars Section, Verse 11):

> कुजः पीडयेल्लाभगोऽपत्यशत्रून् भवेत् संमुखो दुर्मुखोऽपिप्रतापात् ।
> धनं वर्धते गोधनैः वाहनैर्वा सकृच्छून्यतार्थे च पैशुन्यभावात् ॥ ११॥

*Pratapa*, a searing, almost solar valor, gives the native real, undeniable authority. But the verse pairs two words that describe how that authority lands socially: *Sammukha*, direct, someone who faces every obstacle head-on, and *Durmukha*, literally harsh-faced or bad-mouthed. The same directness that makes him formidable is what makes him hard to be around.

## Mars in the 11th House: Wealth and Gains
The verse names *Godhana*, wealth through livestock, dairy, and primary production, and *Vahana*, wealth through vehicles, logistics, or fleets, as the concrete channels. This is not passive or inherited wealth. It is tied to tangible, moving assets and production at scale.

## Mars in the 11th House: Effects on Children and Enemies
The verse's claim about distress to children (*apatya*) and enemies (*shatru*) resolves through *dṛṣṭi*, planetary aspect. Mars in the eleventh casts its 7th-house aspect onto the 5th house, *Suta Bhava*, the house of children, bringing *Pida*, friction or health difficulty, to offspring. It casts its 8th-house aspect onto the 6th house, *Ari Bhava*, the house of open enemies, which is what drives the native's near-total dominance over opposition.

## Mars in the 11th House: The Risk That Erases the Gains
The warning here is specific and severe: *Sakṛcchūnyatārthe*, wealth collapsing suddenly (*sakṛt*) to nothing (*shunyata*). Not a gradual decline. A sharp drop. The verse attributes this squarely to *Paishunya-bhavat*, the habit of backbiting or malicious slander, rooted in *Pishuna*, cruel or wicked speech. Of everything that could threaten this native's prosperity, the text names exactly one cause.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 11th house good or bad for wealth?** Good by default: real gains through livestock and vehicles, but a specific behavioral risk can erase all of it suddenly.
**Does Mars in the 11th house affect children?** Yes, through the 7th-house aspect onto the 5th house of children, bringing friction or health difficulty.
**What can cause sudden financial loss for Mars in the 11th house?** The verse names one specific cause: malicious slander (*Paishunya*), not market conditions or fate.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Mars Section, Verse 11, with standard Jyotish aspect theory (*dṛṣṭi*) applied to the verse's claims about children and enemies.
