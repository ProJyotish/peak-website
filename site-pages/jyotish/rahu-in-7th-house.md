**Quick answer:** Rahu in the 7th house (*Saptamabhava*, marriage and partnership) tends to absorb the damage of this placement into the marriage itself: the text names destruction, painful separation, and public scandal without softening it. Health follows the same agitation, and the native rarely finds real peace, though rivals are genuinely overcome in the process. A Pisces configuration adds a pilgrimage yoga as a counterweight.

## What Rahu in the 7th House Means for Marriage and Partnerships
The *Saptamabhava* is the mirror of the self: not just the spouse, but the native's projection into the world. Bhatta Narayana's *Chamatkara Chintamani*, Rahu Phalam, Verse 7, gives the foundational image for what happens when Rahu, the *chaya graha* of insatiable desire, sits there: *katahe yatha lodayet jataveda*, "like fire churning in a cauldron." *Jataveda* is a Vedic epithet for Agni, the "knower of all births," so this is an ancestral, karmic churning of the *manas* (mind) and *prana* (life force). Because the 7th house is the reflective axis of the Lagna, this internal stirring keeps the native from finding stable ground.

On the partner directly, the verse is explicit: *vinashamlabheyuh*, destruction or loss, is not a quiet affliction. It arrives with *viyoga* (painful separation) and *apavada* (social reproach or outright scandal). The partner's own vitality suffers too, often mirroring the native's internal agitation as chronic health trouble. There is a companion detail worth reading alongside this: Verse 1 of the same text, describing Rahu in the Lagna, notes the native may be *bhuri-daro*, having many partners. Because Rahu in the 1st house casts its shadow directly onto the 7th, the plurality of partners in Verse 1 is often the downstream result of the separation and destruction described here.

## Rahu in the 7th House: Health Effects
The physical toll is named in two specific terms. *Dhatu-paka* is the metabolic decay or "cooking" of the body's essential tissues, here consuming the *Shukra Dhatu* (reproductive vitality) and depleting *Ojas*. *Chandra-mardi* is the affliction of the lunar principle, showing up as fluid imbalance and chronic psychological restlessness.

## Rahu in the 7th House: Personality and Rivalry
The governing phrase is *sharmam na prayanti*, they do not attain peace. The mind is *lodayet*, constantly stirred, with no stillness in it. This is not a placid life, but the native is not without teeth: reflecting the *bhagnariviryah* trait from Verse 1 (breaking the strength of enemies), and given that Rahu in the 7th puts Ketu in the 1st, rivals are genuinely overcome here. It comes at real cost to the native's own peace of mind.

## Rahu in the 7th House in Pisces (Meena)
If Rahu sits in Pisces with Saturn, Mercury, and Mars in Capricorn, the *Bhavartha Ratnakara* records a turn toward purification during the Mars and Rahu Dasas: dips in holy rivers like the Ganga, a counterweight, in the text's own logic, to the cauldron's fire described above.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Rahu in the 7th house bad for marriage?** The classical text is direct about it, naming destruction, separation, and public scandal as the primary marital risks of this placement.
**Does Rahu in the 7th house help against rivals?** Yes, because Rahu in the 7th places Ketu in the 1st, the text associates this axis with genuinely overcoming enemies, though it costs the native's own peace of mind.
**What health issues does Rahu in the 7th house point to?** The text names *dhatu-paka* (tissue decay affecting reproductive vitality) and *chandra-mardi* (fluid imbalance and psychological restlessness).

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani* by Bhatta Narayana (Rahu Phalam, Verses 1, 7, and 12) is the primary source throughout. *Bhavartha Ratnakara* (Chapter 10: Combinations for Holy Dips) supplies the Pisces yoga. The *Brihat Samhita* (Chapter 5, Rahu-cara) and the *Phaladeepika* provide the broader framework for house and shadow-planet significations but are not the source of the 7th-house specifics above.
