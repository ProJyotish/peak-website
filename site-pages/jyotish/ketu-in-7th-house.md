**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 7th house (Saptama Bhava, the house of marriage) brings pain to the spouse, heavy anxiety around travel, a literal fear of water, risk of ruin to the self, and expenses that outrun income. In Scorpio specifically, the entire wealth outcome reverses into "always profitable."

## Ketu in the 7th House: Marriage and Partnership
The controlling text is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Ketu Phala, Verse 7). The seventh house, *Saptama Bhava*, is a *Kendra* (angle) representing the western horizon and, anatomically within the *Kalapurusha*, the genital organ (*Brihat Jataka*, Chapter I, Verse 4). Ketu here, called *Shikhi*, disrupts the house's core business directly. *Kalatradipida*: since the seventh house governs the partner, this names pain and suffering landing specifically on the spouse.

## Ketu in the 7th House: Travel and Movement
*Bhuyasi Margachinta* names heavy anxiety around travel and journeys, a native who cannot settle regarding paths, movement, or being constantly on the road.

## Ketu in the 7th House: Physical Danger
*Varibhiti* is a literal fear of water, danger connected to aquatic environments, stated as a literal risk rather than a metaphor. *Svanasho*, destruction or ruin of the self, is meant literally too: physical or circumstantial ruin, not a modern identity crisis.

## Ketu in the 7th House: Money and Anxiety
*Vyayovyagrata* names excessive expenditure paired with constant mental agitation, money and anxiety feeding each other.

## Ketu in the 7th House in Scorpio (Vrishchika)
*Bhatta Narayana* identifies one specific reversal. When Ketu sits in Scorpio, called *Kitagaha* in the verse (the sign of the *Kita*, the insect or worm), the malefic results are mitigated outright, and Ketu becomes *Sarvadalabhakari*, "always profitable." The native gains financial wealth here instead of the destitution the placement otherwise threatens.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does Ketu in the 7th house cause marriage problems?** Yes, the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* names *Kalatradipida*, pain for the spouse, directly.
**Is there an exception for Ketu in the 7th house?** Yes, in Scorpio specifically, where the placement becomes *Sarvadalabhakari*, always profitable, reversing the financial outcome.
**Does Ketu in the 7th house cause fear of water?** Yes, *Varibhiti* is named as a specific, literal effect of this placement.
