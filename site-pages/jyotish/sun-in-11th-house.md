**Quick answer:** Sun in the 11th house (Ekadasha Bhava, the house of gains) is one of the strongest material placements the Sun gets: real institutional power, income tied to the state, and enemies that don't survive contact with the native's standing. The cost lands specifically on the children, through the Sun's own aspect onto the 5th house.

## Why Sun in the 11th House Works for Gains
This is *Ekadasha Bhava*, the house of *Labha*, gains. The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Surya Phalam, Verse 11), read within the *Upachaya* framework, the classical grouping of the 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 11th houses where a malefic planet's harshness turns productive rather than destructive. The *Phaladeepika* (Chapter 8) ties this principle directly to the strength of this specific placement. Unlike its placement in a *Kendra* or *Trikona*, where the Sun's *Tapa*, heat, can wither domestic or personal significations, Surya in the 11th house redirects that heat outward, burning through obstacles to gain rather than through the native's own comfort.

## Sun in the 11th House: Authority and Income
*Nripadwarato*, wealth through "the royal door," reads in modern terms as income tied to the state: government contracts, political connection, high-tier civil service. *Rajamudradhikara* is more specific than plain employment. It is the compound of *Raja* (royal), *Mudra* (seal), and *Adhikara* (authority): the actual power to validate, to govern, to command institutional resources, not just draw a salary from an institution.

## Sun in the 11th House: Social Dominance
The verse's own image is vivid: *Pratapanale shatravah sampatanti*, enemies falling into the "fire of splendor," consumed by the sheer weight of the native's standing rather than defeated in any conventional contest. The resulting prosperity is described as *Shriyo-anekadha*, manifold, not one lump sum but a diversified spread of status, income, and influence, the kind of position that doesn't depend on any single source holding up.

## Where it gets complicated: children
**The cost lands on the children.** *Duhkham-angodbhavanam*, distress regarding what is born of one's own body, and the text gives the mechanism rather than leaving it vague: the Sun's seventh aspect. Sitting in the 11th house, the Sun casts its full *dṛṣṭi* directly onto the 5th house, *Suta Bhava*, the house of children, and that heat shows up as health trouble, emotional distance, or grief connected to the native's own offspring. The same placement that crushes external rivals runs hot enough to burn something much closer.

Worth flagging separately, since it is a different technique entirely: the *Brihat Samhita* (Chapter 104), on transits rather than birth placement, notes that the Sun's transit through the 11th house is one of the few transit windows read as purely auspicious, without the usual transit-related friction. That is a transit teaching, not a natal one, and it does not cancel out the birth-chart trade-off described above.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Sun in the 11th house good for wealth and gains?** Yes, this is one of the Sun's strongest material placements: real institutional authority and income tied to the state or high office.
**Does Sun in the 11th house affect children?** Yes, the text traces this directly to the Sun's seventh aspect landing on the 5th house of children, causing distress described as *Duhkham-angodbhavanam*.
**Why is a malefic planet good in the 11th house?** Because the 11th is an *Upachaya* house, where a malefic's harshness burns through obstacles to gain instead of damaging personal life, unlike its effect from a *Kendra* or *Trikona*.

## Sources
