**Quick answer:** Jupiter in the 10th house (*Karmasthana*, the zenith of the chart) gives one of the strongest classical career placements: standing in government or sacred institutions, a *Sattvic* and morally disciplined character, and glory the texts say surpasses even the native's ancestors. The direct trade-off named in the same verse is real and lasting dissatisfaction concerning sons.

## Jupiter in the 10th House: Career and Public Standing
The 10th is a *Kendra* (angle), the zenith of the chart and the native's visible footprint in the world. The *Chamatkara Chintamani* opens its treatment of Jupiter by describing him as *Devamantri*, Minister of the Gods, dressed in radiant yellow (*pitapattambaram*), the most refined *Sattva* in the system. Placed at the zenith, that dignity projects onto the native's field of action. Verse 10 is specific about where this authority lands: the native finds standing in *Dhwajamandape*, seats of government, and in *Mandire*, temples and sacred institutions, functioning in both as a custodian of order. Association with the *Chitrashala*, the halls of the distinguished, follows. The single most potent line in the verse is *Tejoadhikattvam*: a radiance and influence that surpasses not just the father but every ancestor who came before (*Pithuh purvajebhyo api*). Effort, described as *Siddhido*, is naturally successful here, converting into tangible, high-status results.

## Jupiter in the 10th House: Personality and Character
The personality matches the office. *Brihat Jataka* and *Phaladeepika* both point to a *Sattvic* constitution: profound religious inclination, high moral standing, an intellect that is wise rather than merely clever, aimed at preserving tradition and the collective good. This is a native who embodies the moral weight of a counselor, earning respect through disciplined virtue rather than demanding it through the office itself.

## Jupiter in the 10th House: Sons
*"Na tushtobhavechhamana putrakano"*: the *Chamatkara Chintamani* states, without qualification, a lack of satisfaction, happiness, or mental peace regarding sons. This sits directly beside the career results above, not after them. Whatever the professional luster, the quality or conduct of male offspring remains a source of ongoing internal discontent, stated as a paired fact rather than an escapable footnote.

## Jupiter in the 10th House: Daily Discipline
The *Chamatkara Chintamani* also records a specific lifestyle detail: *Pachet pratyaham prasthasamudramannam*, a daily measured intake of food, a *Prastha* (roughly 64 *tolas*). Read as more than diet, this is close to a ritual requirement, a way of sustaining the *Tejas* (radiance) that the native's high-status duties demand.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Jupiter in the 10th house good for career?** Yes, this is considered one of the strongest classical placements for career, status, and institutional authority.
**Does Jupiter in the 10th house affect relationships with sons?** Yes, and directly: the texts name a lasting lack of satisfaction concerning sons alongside the placement's career strengths.
**What kind of career suits Jupiter in the 10th house?** Government, religious institutions, law, and any advisory role built on moral authority rather than raw ambition.

## Sources
