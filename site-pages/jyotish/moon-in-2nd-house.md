**Quick answer:** Moon in the 2nd house (Dhana Bhava, the house of wealth and speech) gives steady material comfort, a magnetic physical presence, and deep personal contentment, per *Chamatkara Chintaamani*. The same source names a direct cost: the bond with one's own family and lineage runs thin. For Virgo (Kanya) Lagna specifically, a rare Dhana Yoga can form with Moon and Venus in Libra.

## Moon in the 2nd House: Wealth Effects
The 2nd house is *Vasu-sthana*, built on *Vasu*, the eight elemental spheres that ground material existence. *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Chapter 2, Verse 2) describes Chandra here, invoked as *Himanshu*, the cool-rayed one, as a placement of fluid, sustaining wealth rather than dramatic windfalls: the general result is *dhanya-labha*, a continuous gain of grains and prosperity.

## Moon in the 2nd House: Personality and Charisma
The same verse describes *sharire'ti saukhyam*, great comfort of the body, and *Ati-saukhyam*, a state of deep psychic contentment: because the 2nd house governs what a person "takes in," the native is described as perpetually, internally satisfied. It goes further into presence: *Vasham darshane yaati devangana'pi*, even celestial women come under the native's influence simply by sight, a claim about visual magnetism strong enough to cross ordinary social boundaries. *Vilaso-angananam* adds a life of romantic enjoyment and refined pleasure.

## Moon in the 2nd House and Family Bonds
Against all that comfort and charisma, *Chamatkara Chintaamani* states plainly: *Kutumbe ratih jayate tasya tuccham*. The attachment or pleasure the native draws from the *Kutumba*, the family unit, is meager, hollow, insignificant. That is not a soft caveat, it is a direct statement that the same self-contained contentment that makes this native so at ease in their own skin leaves comparatively little left over for the ancestral, domestic bond the 2nd house is also supposed to represent.

## Moon in the 2nd House in Libra (Tula): The Kanya Lagna Dhana Yoga
The *Bhavartha Ratnakara* preserves a narrow, specific wealth combination worth flagging for a Kanya (Virgo) Lagna chart: Moon and Venus in Libra (the 2nd house from that Lagna), with Jupiter and Mercury in Pisces (the 7th), forms a potent *Dhana Yoga*. Moon and Venus act as the *karakas* here, but the fruition is timed specifically to the **Jupiter Dasha**, not to the Moon's own period. The placement sets the potential up; something else has to trigger it.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Moon in the 2nd house good for wealth?** Generally yes, giving steady rather than dramatic gains, per *Chamatkara Chintaamani*.
**Does Moon in the 2nd house affect family relationships?** Yes, the text names a specific weakness in the bond to one's own *Kutumba* (family unit), despite personal comfort being high.
**What is the Dhana Yoga for Moon in the 2nd house?** A Virgo (Kanya) Lagna-specific combination: Moon and Venus in Libra, Jupiter and Mercury in Pisces, fruiting in the Jupiter Dasha.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Chapter 2, Verse 2. *Bhavartha Ratnakara* (the Kanya Lagna Dhana Yoga and its Jupiter Dasha timing).
