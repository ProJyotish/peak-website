**Quick answer:** Mars in the 4th house (Chaturtha Bhava, the house of home and heart) buys real material standing, favor from authority, land, and fine living, at the direct cost of the comfort a home is supposed to provide. Friends bring no happiness, kin relationships run toward separation, and the heat of Mars lands specifically on the heart and chest.

## What Mars in the 4th House Means for Home and Domestic Life
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Bhauma Phalam, Verse 4), which opens with a rhetorical challenge: when *Bhusuta* (Mars, son of the earth) sits in the fourth house, what use are the other planets, even the favorable ones? Happiness from *Suhrit-varga*, the circle of friends, the text says, is "not to be contemplated." Some descriptions of an *"Ashantam"* (never-peaceful) mind or a lion-like (*Kanthirava*) dominance of the home are sometimes attached to this placement by analogy to other verses in the same text, but the fourth-house verse itself speaks directly only to the loss of friendship's happiness.

## Mars in the 4th House: Property and Material Gains
The *Chamatkara Chintamani* is specific about material gain here: *Bhumipala-Kripa*, favor from the ruling power (in practical terms, government approval, regulatory goodwill, high-level patronage), *Vastra*, fine clothing and material standard of living, and *Bhumi*, real gains in land and fixed property.

## Mars in the 4th House: Family and Kin
*Bandhu-viyoga*, separation or distancing from kin, is named directly, and support from the *Kula* (lineage) tends to be absent or actively contested rather than simply thin. Combined with the denial of *Suhrit-varga* happiness, the picture is consistent: material security purchased in a home that doesn't feel emotionally secured.

## Mars in the 4th House: Health Effects
The *Brihat Jataka* (Chapter 1, Verse 3) maps the fourth house to *Hridaya*, the heart, and the breast, within the *Kalapurusha* (the cosmic body the zodiac is read through). The heat of Mars lands specifically on this region, bringing *Pida*, pain or affliction, to the heart and chest. The general Martian risks of *Agni* (fire) and *Loha* (iron) apply as background factors, but this localized affliction is the primary, house-specific risk.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 4th house good or bad for home life?** Mixed. Material gains from property and authority are real, but domestic and social happiness are explicitly denied.
**Does Mars in the 4th house affect family relationships?** Yes, the text names *Bandhu-viyoga*, separation from kin, directly.
**Does Mars in the 4th house bring property or material gains?** Yes: land (*Bhumi*), fine clothing (*Vastra*), and favor from authority (*Bhumipala-Kripa*) are all named.

## Sources
*Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verses 3 and 5. *Chamatkara Chintamani*, Bhauma, Verse 4. Note on scope: *Phaladeepika* was checked for this house, and the available source material did not contain a specific fourth-house Mars verse from it, so it isn't cited here.
