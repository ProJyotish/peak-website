**Quick answer:** Mars in the 7th house (Saptam Bhava, the house of marriage, trade, and travel) is one of the classical Mangal Dosha placements. The text names outright damage to the couple's harmony, while the same energy gives the native a real edge over business rivals and a life of travel that never quite feels finished. Physical risk from blows, iron, and fire runs through the placement as well.

## What Mars in the 7th House Means for Marriage and Mangal Dosha
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Verse 7). The verse names the marital result *Dampatighnah*, the destruction of the couple's harmony, and *Muhurbhagadah*, repeated setbacks and breakages of peace. The native's disposition toward partnership is described as *Anuddharabhutena*, an unlifted, un-redeemable burden, language that treats the union itself as weight rather than support.

> अनुद्धारभूतेन पाणिग्रहेण... प्रहारार्दनैः सप्तमे दम्पतिघ्नः ॥ ७॥
> *Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Verse 7

This is one of the more direct warnings against a comfortable marriage anywhere in the classical material, and it is the textual basis for treating Mars in the 7th as a classic Mangal Dosha placement, even though the source itself uses *Dampatighnah* rather than that later term.

## Mars in the 7th House: Business and Trade
In trade, the same energy reads differently, and better, for the native's own position. *Spardhinam*, hostility toward competitors, produces real damage to rivals, structural setbacks the text calls *Bhagadah*. But the underlying condition is *No nivruttih*, no cessation. There is no point where the struggle in commerce actually resolves into peaceful gain. The native wins rounds. The fight itself does not end.

## Mars in the 7th House: Travel
The same *no nivruttih* condition governs *Prayana*, travel. The native moves often, but these journeys do not conclude the way travel is supposed to, with a sense of completion. It is closer to wandering than to arriving.

## Mars in the 7th House: Physical Risk
*Praharardanaih* names a real probability of blows, sudden strokes, or accident-driven injury. This sits alongside the general Martian risk vocabulary from Verse 1, *Loha-agni-danda-bhiti*, fear connected to iron, fire, and *Danda* (legal or authoritative punishment), which the text locates most strongly in the native's public and external dealings rather than at home.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 7th house good or bad for marriage?** Poor by the text's own account. *Dampatighnah* names direct damage to the couple's harmony, with repeated setbacks rather than stability.
**Does Mars in the 7th house cause Mangal Dosha?** Yes, this is one of the classical Mangal Dosha houses, and the described effect (damage to the couple's harmony) matches the substance of the dosha even without the source using that exact term.
**Does Mars in the 7th house help in business?** Yes, it gives a real edge over competitors, though the struggle in trade itself never fully resolves.

## Sources
