**Quick answer:** Rahu in the 6th house (*Ripubhavam*, the house of enemies) is one of the genuinely favorable placements for Rahu: a steadiness of mind the text says has no equal, used to overpower rivals and convert that victory into wealth. Physical constitution is robust, and results are tied primarily to the Rahu Dasa, with a Pisces configuration adding a pilgrimage yoga.

## What Rahu in the 6th House Means for the Mind and Enemies
The source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* of Bhatta Narayana, Verse 6:

> वलम् बुधिवीर्यं धनं तद्वशेन स्थितो वैरिभावेऽयेषां जनानाम् ।
> रिपूणामरण्यं दहेदेव राहुः स्थिरं मानसं तत्तुला नो पृथिव्याम् ॥ ६ ॥
> *"Valam budhi-veeryam dhanam tadvashina sthitho vairibhave'yeshaam janaanaam | Ripoonam-aranam dahedeva Rahuh sthiram maanasam tattuulaa no pruthivyaam ||"*

*Sthiram Manasam*, a steady or fixed mind, is the anchor of this placement. The verse is not modest about it: *tattuulaa no pruthivyaam*, there is nothing on earth to compare it to. What makes this more than a claim of stubbornness is the pairing with *Budhi-veeryam*, intellect fused with valor, so the native's courage comes out strategic as much as brave. The 6th house is *Ripubhavam*, the house of enemies, and Rahu here is described with a specific, violent image: *Ripoonam-aranam dahedeva*, burning the forest of enemies. *Ripoonam* is "of enemies," *aranam* is forest or thicket. However dense or numerous the opposition, the metaphor holds that Rahu clears it entirely, functioning like a consuming fire directed outward, away from the self and onto whatever stands against it.

## Rahu in the 6th House: Wealth and Career
The Chintaamani makes an explicit promise of *Dhanam*, wealth, and is specific about where it comes from. It is not windfall. It is *tadvashina*, produced through the same intellectual power and strategic valor already described, arriving as adversaries are subdued rather than through any external stroke of fortune.

## Rahu in the 6th House: Health and Physical Strength
The *Brihat Jataka* (Ch. 1, Verse 4) maps the 6th house to the navel, *Nabhi*, of the *Kalapurusha*. Consistent with the rest of this placement, the physical picture is one of strength: *Valam* names real physical robustness, a constitution able to withstand the pressure that comes built into a house whose entire business is opposition. The *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* (Chapter 36) places the results of this placement formally under the results of the Rahu Dasa, meaning the timing frame for these effects is tied primarily to Rahu's own major period.

## Rahu in the 6th House in Pisces (Meena)
A separate, sign-specific note applies if Rahu occupies Pisces here: with Saturn, Mercury, and Mars in Capricorn, the *Bhavartha Ratnakara* records dips in holy rivers like the Ganga, specifically during the Dasas of Mars and Rahu.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Rahu in the 6th house a good placement?** Yes, it is one of the classically favorable placements for Rahu, associated with an unmatched steadiness of mind and real success against enemies.
**Does Rahu in the 6th house bring wealth?** Yes, the text promises *Dhanam* directly, produced through strategic intellect rather than luck, as competitors are overcome.
**When do the effects of Rahu in the 6th house fruit?** Primarily during the Rahu Dasa, per the *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*, with a separate pilgrimage yoga during the Mars and Rahu Dasas if Rahu sits in Pisces.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Bhatta Narayana), Verse 6, for the core signature of mind, enemies, and wealth. *Brihat Jataka* (Ch. 1, Verse 4), for the navel as the 6th house's anatomical seat. *Bhavartha Ratnakara* (Ch. 1, Point 11), for the Pisces pilgrimage configuration. *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* (Chapter 36), for the Dasa-result categorization.
