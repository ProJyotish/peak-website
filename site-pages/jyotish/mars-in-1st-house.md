*Analysis based on traditional Jyotish texts. Full citations at the end of this page.*

**Quick answer:** Mars in the 1st house (Tanu Bhava, the house of the body) gives a bold, dominating temperament the texts compare to a lion, alongside chronic mental agitation traced to trouble with spouse and kin. Health risk centers on the head and eyes, with named dangers from iron and fire. The same placement aspects the 7th house directly, striking at the marriage. In Aries (Mesha) lagna, where Mars rules its own sign, the placement gains real strength, and a specific Mars-Venus-Jupiter yoga can bring fortune during Mercury and Jupiter Dasas.

## What Mars in the 1st House Means for Personality and Temperament
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Mars Section, Verse 1), which asks *"Kesari kim dvitiyah"*, is he a second lion? It is not a compliment so much as a description: a bold, authoritative presence that brooks no rivals. But the same verse names the cost. The mind carries *Tapen Manasam*, a persistent heat or agitation, and the text is specific about its source: *"daradayada-vargat"*, from the circle of spouse, kinsmen, and heirs. This is domestic in origin, not free-floating anxiety.

## Mars in the 1st House: Health Effects
Mangal in the Lagna brings *Shira-Netra-Pida*, chronic trouble in the head and eyes, since Mars sits in the sign the *Brihat Jataka* (1.4) maps to the head of the *Kalapurusha* (the cosmic body the zodiac is read through). The *Vipake-phalanam*, the fruit of the planet's influence as it ripens, is marked by *Upasarga*, obstacles and calamity. The text names three specific fears: *Danda* (conflict with authority or the law), *Loha* (injury from iron or weapons), and *Agni* (fire, heat, scalds).

## Mars in the 1st House: Marriage and Family
Mars in the 1st aspects the 7th house directly, and the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* calls the result *Kalatradi-ghatah*, a blow or striking injury to the spouse. Because Mangal sits in *Tanu* (the self) and looks straight across at *Kalatra* (the spouse), the native's own temperament becomes the force that lands on the marriage.

## Mars in the 1st House: Career and Fortune
The general pattern is hard-fought victories against persistent *Upasarga* rather than an easy run. The *Bhavartha Ratnakara* (Section 1.10) records an exception: if Mars is joined by Venus in the Lagna while Jupiter sits in Capricorn, real fortune arrives, specifically during the Dasas of Mercury and Jupiter. The text flags this as a named exception to the ordinary rules of debility, since Jupiter is debilitated in Capricorn in this same configuration.

## Mars in the 1st House in Aries (Mesha) Lagna
Mars rules Mesha (Aries) and Vrischika (Scorpio), per the *Brihat Jataka* (1.6). When Mars sits in the 1st house in its own sign, Aries, its rulership aligns with its placement at the head of the *Kalapurusha*: the native becomes what the source calls a living manifestation of the planet's unshielded power, with courage and conflict as the defining thread of the life path.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 1st house good or bad?** Neither. It gives real courage and presence, paired with chronic health risk and domestic friction.
**Does Mars in the 1st house cause Mangal Dosha?** The 1st house is one of the traditional Dosha houses, and the text's own description of Mars aspecting the 7th and striking the spouse (*Kalatradi-ghatah*) is the same effect Mangal Dosha describes, even though the source doesn't use that term.
**What is the yoga for fortune with Mars in the 1st house?** Mars joined by Venus in the Lagna, with Jupiter in Capricorn, brings fortune specifically in Mercury and Jupiter Dasas.
