Everything here is drawn from classical Jyotish texts, Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, Bhavartha Ratnakara, and Phaladeepika among them, not general-purpose horoscope writing. Each page cites the verse it comes from.

Two structures are covered in full.

## The ascendants

What the rising sign itself gives a native: temperament, body, and the yogas specific to that lagna.

<details>
<summary>All 12 ascendants</summary>

- **[Aries ascendant](/jyotish/aries-ascendant):** Aries ascendant personality and traits from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Mars rulership, health tendencies, and dasha yogas.
- **[Taurus ascendant](/jyotish/taurus-ascendant):** Taurus ascendant traits and temperament from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Venus rulership, the Moon exception, and Saturn as yogakaraka.
- **[Gemini ascendant](/jyotish/gemini-ascendant):** Gemini ascendant traits from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Mercury rulership, intellect and speech, and dasha-specific wealth yogas.
- **[Cancer ascendant](/jyotish/cancer-ascendant):** Cancer ascendant personality from Brihat Jataka, Phaladeepika, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Moon rulership, temperament, and the lunar wealth paradox.
- **[Leo ascendant](/jyotish/leo-ascendant):** Leo ascendant traits from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Sun rulership, Sun versus Moon temperament, and dasha-specific yogas.
- **[Virgo ascendant](/jyotish/virgo-ascendant):** Virgo ascendant personality from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Mercury rulership, physical traits, professions, and wealth yogas.
- **[Libra ascendant](/jyotish/libra-ascendant):** Libra ascendant traits from Brihat Jataka and Chamatkara Chintamani: planet-by-planet effects in the lagna, health tendencies, and Bhavartha Ratnakara wealth yogas.
- **[Scorpio ascendant](/jyotish/scorpio-ascendant):** Scorpio ascendant personality from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Mars rulership, temperament, health tendencies, and wealth yogas.
- **[Sagittarius ascendant](/jyotish/sagittarius-ascendant):** Sagittarius ascendant traits from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Jupiter rulership, temperament, planet-in-lagna effects, and dasha yogas.
- **[Capricorn ascendant](/jyotish/capricorn-ascendant):** Capricorn ascendant personality from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Saturn rulership, temperament, planet-in-lagna effects, and rajayoga.
- **[Aquarius ascendant](/jyotish/aquarius-ascendant):** Aquarius ascendant traits from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Saturn rulership, temperament, health tendencies, and wealth yogas.
- **[Pisces ascendant](/jyotish/pisces-ascendant):** Pisces ascendant personality from Brihat Jataka, Chamatkara Chintamani, and Bhavartha Ratnakara: Jupiter rulership, temperament, and the Moon's wealth contradiction.

</details>

## Planets through the houses

What each planet gives when placed in each house, 108 combinations in total.

- **[3rd house](/jyotish/sun-in-3rd-house):** Sun in the 3rd house (Tritiya Bhava) effects on courage, siblings, enemies, and pilgrimage, drawn from the classical Jyotish text Chamatkara Chintaamani.
- **[4th house](/jyotish/sun-in-4th-house):** Sun in the 4th house (Caturtha Bhava) effects on home, family conflict, foreign residence, and inner peace, drawn from the classical texts Chamatkara Chintamani and Brihat Jataka.
- **[5th house](/jyotish/sun-in-5th-house):** Sun in the 5th house (Suta-sthana) effects on intelligence, children, ethics, and health, drawn from the classical Jyotish text Chamatkara Chintaamani.
- **[6th house](/jyotish/sun-in-6th-house):** Sun in the 6th house (Ripusthana) effects on defeating enemies, money drains, and travel risk, from Chamatkara Chintamani, Brihat Samhita, and Brihat Jataka.

- **[9th house](/jyotish/sun-in-9th-house):** Sun in the 9th house (Dharma Bhava) effects on fortune, mental restlessness, forced asceticism, and career eminence, from the classical text Chamatkara Chintaamani.

- **[12th house](/jyotish/sun-in-12th-house):** Sun in the 12th house (Vyaya Bhava) effects on eye health, hard-won victories, and status that dissolves, from the classical text Chamatkara Chintamani.
