**Quick answer:** Mars in the 12th house (Vyaya Bhava, the house of expenditure and loss) turns a planet that is normally aggressive outward into something that consumes the native from within. The text names active destruction of wealth, real risk of eye injury and wounds from iron, and a predatory temperament that returns as false rumors and constant discord. The classical record for this specific house is thinner than for any other Mangal placement: a single verse carries the entire weight.

## What Mars in the 12th House Means for Wealth and Loss
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Mars Section, Verse 12). The verse names *Arthasya-nasham*, the destruction of wealth, in active terms: this isn't slow leakage, it's the burning-through of resources. Alongside it sits *Dasyu-bhayam*, fear of thieves and outlaws, tying financial loss to external predation as much as to the native's own habits.

## Mars in the 12th House: Health Effects
The verse reaches for hyperbole to make its point about this house's traditional link to the eyes: *Shataksho'pi tat sakshato*, even a being with a hundred eyes would find every one of them wounded under this influence. Alongside that sits a plainer risk, *Loha-ghataih*, injury from iron implements or weaponry, and a blunt naming of the overall effect: *Duhkha-hetu*, the cause of sorrow, applied to both the physical pain and the psychological distress this placement produces.

## Mars in the 12th House: Reputation and Temperament
*Mrisha-kimvadanti*, exposure to false rumors and scandal, describes a native whose public reputation takes damage regardless of his actual conduct. The verse's sharpest psychological term is *Paradhi*, hunter or predator, used to describe a disposition inclined to exploit or harm others. That predatory instinct doesn't stay contained. It returns as *Kali*, the quarrels and discord that keep the native's own domestic and social environment unsettled.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 12th house good or bad?** Poor by the text's own account. This is one of the more difficult placements in the Mangal material, with no dignity clause or exception yoga recorded for it.
**Does Mars in the 12th house affect health?** Yes, specifically the eyes, and real risk of injury from iron implements or weapons.
**Does Mars in the 12th house affect reputation?** Yes, the text names exposure to false rumors and scandal directly.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Mars Section, Verse 12. Note on scope: the *Brihat Samhita*, *Brihat Jataka*, *Phaladeepika*, and *Bhavartha Ratnakara* were consulted for this house but contain no specific verse-level treatment of Mars in the twelfth house in the material available, so this page relies on the single citation above.
