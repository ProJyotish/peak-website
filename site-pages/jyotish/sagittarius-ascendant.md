**Quick answer:** The Sagittarius ascendant is ruled by Jupiter but rises feet first, *Prishtodaya*, so despite the wisdom-granting rulership, results here arrive slowly and are genuinely earned. The native is philosophical and scholarly by temperament, with a body split between human aspiration above and grounded, horse-like drive below.

## Fundamental Identity: Jupiter, the Thighs, and the Centaur

Dhanu (Sagittarius), the ninth sign, is ruled by Jupiter (*Guru*, *Brihat Jataka*, Ch. 1, Stanza 5), a night sign (Stanza 10) whose feet-first rising traditionally signals delay and real struggle in the early stages of any undertaking. The image is a centaur, human above and horse below, representing the two thighs of the *Kalapurusha* (Stanza 4).

## Temperament: The Scholar's Sign

Jupiter as *Deva-Guru*, preceptor of the gods, brings a genuinely philosophical and scholarly dimension. Varahamihira frames his own *Brihat Jataka*, in its preface, as a small boat meant to carry "intelligent students" and "able men" across the ocean of horoscopy, and the Dhanu native is close to the natural reader that line describes: built to synthesize scripture and philosophy into usable wisdom.

## Behavioral Tendencies: A Slow, Hard-Won Path

The *Prishtodaya* nature shapes the whole trajectory. Unlike *Shirshodaya* (head-first) signs, where success can arrive early, Dhanu's path demands the foundation be laid properly first, with progress hard-won rather than handed over.

## Body and Bearing by Planet in the Lagna

- **Sun:** a tall, upright body (*Tunga-yashtim*), consistently afflicted by wind and bile (*Vapuh pidyate vatapittena*). Mental distress centers on spouse and kinsmen, alongside constant travel and a status that cycles between rise and fall.
- **Moon** (*Gokuliraja-gah*, occupying Sagittarius or Taurus): handsomeness and attractiveness, a *Dhanadhyaksha* quality, lord of wealth, and a life closer to bliss, a departure from the Moon's more difficult effects elsewhere.
- **Mars** (*Kuja*): injuries to the head and eyes, and the specific triad of fears, *Danda-loha-agni-bhiti*, punishment, iron, and fire. The mind runs agitated, tasks meet real obstacles, and distress runs through the spouse.

## Special Yogas for Sagittarius Ascendant (Bhavartha Ratnakara)

- **The Moon's caution:** despite the general rule that the Moon in the lagna of any sign other than Taurus makes the native very fortunate (Point 15), for Dhanu specifically the Moon in the first house is not auspicious and afflicts other *Dhana Yogas* in the chart (Point 14).
- **Fortunate dasa:** Mars and Venus in the lagna with Jupiter in Capricorn brings significant fortune during the *dasas* of Mercury and Jupiter (Point 10).
- **Sacred dips:** Saturn, Mercury, and Mars in Capricorn with Rahu in Pisces bring ritual dips in holy rivers such as the Ganga during the *dasas* of Mars and Rahu (Point 11).
- **Dhana Yoga:** Moon and Venus in Libra with Jupiter and Mercury in Pisces produces a powerful wealth combination during the Jupiter *dasa* (Point 12).
- **Venus dasha wealth:** the Venus *dasa* is named generally as a period of vast wealth accumulation (Point 13).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the personality traits of a Sagittarius ascendant?**
A Sagittarius ascendant is philosophical and scholarly, ruled by Jupiter, with a slow, hard-won path to success shaped by the sign's feet-first rising.

**Is the Moon a good placement for a Sagittarius ascendant?**
No, despite the general rule elsewhere. For Dhanu specifically, the Moon in the first house is not auspicious and afflicts wealth combinations in the chart.

**Which dasha brings wealth for a Sagittarius ascendant?**
The Venus *dasa* is named directly for wealth accumulation, and the Mercury and Jupiter *dasas* also turn fortunate under a Mars-Venus-Jupiter configuration.
