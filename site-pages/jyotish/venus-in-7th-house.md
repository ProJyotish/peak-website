**Quick answer:** Venus in the 7th house (Kalatra Bhava, its own natural house of partnership) gives a spouse described as gem-like, a genuine source of happiness, while the native's own path stays migratory, marked by travel or life away from the birthplace. The same placement produces real public magnetism, effort-based career success, and a physical constitution stabilized by angular strength.

## Venus in the 7th House: Marriage and the Central Paradox
The source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* of Bhatta Narayana, its verse for Venus in the *Saptamabhava*, the seventh house, also called *Kalatra Bhava*, the house of partnership. The native gets a spouse described as *Ratnagarbha*, gem-like, a source of genuine treasure and virtue, and that partner delivers real happiness, *Sukham*. At the same time, the native's path is *Pravasi*, migratory, marked by travel or residence away from the birthplace. The spouse becomes the anchor for a life that otherwise will not sit still. The text captures this with a specific piece of wordplay: *Kalatre kalatrat sukham nokalatrat*, happiness comes from the person of the spouse, not necessarily from the marital home or location itself. The marriage is a portable treasure. It is not, in this reading, a fixed hearth.

## Venus in the 7th House: Public Magnetism
The seventh house is the public face, the counterpart to the first house's private self, and Venus here produces a native who draws real attention. The text asks the rhetorical question *Ke na muhyati tasmat*, who is not fascinated by this person? The term *Vilasadhiko* describes a surplus of luxury and refined, even hedonistic, indulgence. The native does not merely enjoy comfort; they are defined by an excess of it, and that excess is precisely what makes them magnetic.

## Venus in the 7th House: Career and the Price of Success
Success here requires *Prayasa*, deliberate and sustained labor. Nothing about this placement suggests instant results. The *Pravasi* signature carries into the professional sphere too: careers built in foreign lands, or in industries that require constant movement, are the natural outlet for this placement. On timing, Venus Dashas are noted elsewhere as periods conducive to *Dhana Yoga* (*Bhavartha Ratnakara*, Chapter 12, Point 13); in a seventh-house context, that wealth-building tends to run through the native's social capital and the spouse's own influence rather than through solitary effort.

## Venus in the 7th House: Health
Venus sitting in a *Kendra* (angular house) carries real *Kendra-bala*, angular strength, and this stabilizes the native's physical constitution. The state described is *Sharma*, comfort and well-being, functioning almost as a protective shield that lets the native sustain a *Vilasadhiko* lifestyle without it costing them their health.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Venus in the 7th house good for marriage?** Yes, this is Venus's own natural house of partnership, and the classical text describes a spouse of exceptional virtue and value.
**Does Venus in the 7th house cause relocation or travel?** The placement carries a migratory signature, with the native's path marked by residence away from the birthplace or a life spent in motion.
**Does Venus in the 7th house bring wealth easily?** No, the text is specific that success requires deliberate, sustained effort rather than arriving without labor.
