**Quick answer:** Mercury in the 8th house (Randhra Bhava, normally the most feared house in the chart) is treated unusually generously by the classical text: it promises a near-full lifespan, accumulated wealth and fame that reaches into foreign lands, and a playful, affectionate personality that survives sitting in the chart's most serious house.

## Mercury in the 8th House: Longevity
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Verse 8, in the Bhattanarayana recension with the commentary of Malaviyadaivajna Dharmeshwara, opens by calling Mercury *Rajaputre*, the Prince, and carries that youthful, adaptable character into a house that usually punishes exactly those qualities. The central promise is *Shatañjīvino*: a full lifespan, effectively a hundred years, *Purnayu*. The *Randhra Bhava* is normally read as a point where life force can leak away; here, Mercury's flexible, youthful nature is read as converting that same vulnerability into a reservoir.

## Mercury in the 8th House: Career, Wealth, and Fame
Financially, the text names *Nidhānaṃ*, accumulated wealth or treasure, distinct from ordinary monthly income. It arrives by two routes: *Nṛpad*, through the state or royal favor, or *Vikrayād*, through trade and the skillful sale of goods. Layered onto that is *Deśāntare Viśrutaste*, fame that extends into foreign lands, well beyond the native's place of birth.

## Mercury in the 8th House: Personality and Pleasures
The personality read is *Yuvatyudbhavaṃ Krīḍanaṃ Prītimantaḥ*: an affectionate nature that finds real pleasure in play, often in the company of young women. *Krīḍanam* here means more than idleness. It is Mercury's essential character, youthful and communicative, refusing to be flattened by a house normally read as heavy and secretive. The native stays socially capable and charming even sitting in the chart's most serious room.

A scope note worth naming plainly: this reading holds to the verse itself, without layering in aspects, planetary dignity, or conjunctions, since the source text does not reference them for this specific placement. A real chart still needs those checked before leaning on this fully.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does Mercury in the 8th house shorten lifespan?** No, this specific verse promises the opposite: a near-full, hundred-year lifespan.
**Does Mercury in the 8th house bring fame abroad?** Yes, the text names fame that specifically extends into foreign lands.
**Is Mercury in the 8th house a difficult placement?** Not by this reading. The 8th house is usually feared, but the classical verse treats Mercury here as unusually favorable.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Bhattanarayana recension, Budha-Stana, Verse 8, with the commentary of Malaviyadaivajna Dharmeshwara (longevity, wealth, fame, personality).
