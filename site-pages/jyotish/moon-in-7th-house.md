**Quick answer:** Moon in the 7th house (Kalatra Bhava, the house of marriage and partnership) gives happiness through the spouse, a pleasure-seeking temperament, and wealth through water-related trade, but everything favorable here belongs specifically to a waxing Moon. A waning Moon at birth flips the same placement into physical frailty and vulnerability to rivals, per *Chamatkara Chintaamani*.

## Moon in the 7th House: Marriage and Partnership
The 7th house maps to the genital organ of the *Kalapurusha* (*Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Stanza 4) and governs marriage, sexual union, and partnership, along with trade and general social exchange (*Phaladeepika*, Chapter 10). *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Ath-Chandra-Phal, Verse 7) states directly: "The native with the Moon in the 7th house has a lean body, a covetous mind, and is a consumer of sweet foods. While they find happiness through their partner, they may also be susceptible to being overpowered by opponents." Where the Moon has strength, it gives *dade darasham*, happiness through the spouse, a genuinely benefic result. But the same verse pairs that with a caution: the native can be *vipaksha-abhibhutah*, overpowered by enemies or opponents, particularly when the Moon lacks digits.

## Moon in the 7th House: Temperament
The mind here is *lubdha-cheta*, covetous or greedy, paired with a strong taste for *mishta-bhuk*, sweet food, a pairing the text does not dress up. Socially, the native carries *ratim strijane*, an indulgent, pleasure-seeking nature, generally comfortable seeking delight in company.

## Moon in the 7th House: Career and Wealth
Because Chandra is *apya-graha*, a watery planet, its presence in the house of trade points economic effort toward water. *Chamatkara Chintaamani* names *dhanitvam bhaved dhva vanijyato'pi*, wealth through water-based or maritime commerce, alongside general success in buying, selling, and partnership.

## Moon in the 7th House: The Waxing vs. Waning Moon Split
This is the placement's real hinge. Everything favorable above belongs to the Shukla Paksha, the waxing Moon: a waxing or full Moon here produces robustness, wealth, and real marital happiness, because the Moon's benefic rays reach full strength in the *Kalatra Bhava*. Born under a Krishna Paksha, waning Moon, and the same placement reads differently. *Krishah*, the lean or frail body, is named as a literal symptom of low lunar vitality, correlating directly with the native's difficulty withstanding opposition. A native with this placement should know which half of the lunar month they were born into before assuming which version of this house they are getting.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Moon in the 7th house good for marriage?** Yes, when the Moon is waxing. A waning Moon at birth weakens both the marital benefit and the native's resilience against rivals.
**Does Moon in the 7th house affect career?** Yes, specifically pointing toward water-based or maritime trade and general commercial partnership.
**Does the lunar phase matter for Moon in the 7th house?** Yes, decisively. The text bifurcates nearly every result here by whether the Moon was waxing or waning at birth.
