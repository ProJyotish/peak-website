**Quick answer:** Sun in the 6th house (Ripusthana, the house of enemies) is one of the Sun's stronger placements: a consistent, repeatable ability to defeat rivals and opponents. The cost is real too: expenditure through government and friends, misfortune touching the maternal family, distress during travel, and a genuine emotional toll even in victory.

## What Sun in the 6th House Means for Conflict and Authority
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Surya Phalam, Verse 6), read alongside the *Brihat Samhita* and *Brihat Jataka* for the Sun's general nature. *Ripudhwamsakrid*, a destroyer of enemies: the verse describes this as a consistent pattern, not a lucky break. The native carries real solar authority into professional and social conflict, and generally comes out ahead. This is one of the placements where the Sun's harsher qualities, the ones that cause trouble elsewhere in the chart, work exactly as intended.

## Sun in the 6th House: Financial Costs
None of this arrives free. *Tanoti vyayam rajato mitrato'pi*: expenditure flows out through two specific channels, the state (in practical terms, taxes, legal fees, government-mandated costs) and friends (social ties that drain money rather than build it). Two very different kinds of relationship, the impersonal state and the personal friendship, and the verse holds both responsible for the same outflow. *Kule maturapad chatushpadatova* names a further vulnerability on the maternal side of the family, alongside a specific risk involving four-legged animals.

## Sun in the 6th House: Travel
*Prayane nishadaih vishadam karoti*, distress on the road from outsiders, historically described as forest-dwellers or thieves (*Nishadaih*). Journeys into unfamiliar territory don't come easy here.

## Where it gets complicated
*Vishadam*, sorrow, is the word the verse keeps returning to, and it earns attention precisely because everything else about this placement reads as strong. **The native wins the professional and social conflicts. The winning doesn't bring peace.** Triumph over rivals carries a genuine emotional cost here, named in the same breath as the victory rather than tucked away as an afterthought.

There is a mitigating factor worth knowing: strength in the *Shadvarga*, the six-fold divisional chart system, when the Sun sits in its own sign or a dignified one across those divisions, sharpens the enemy-conquering side of the placement and eases the *Vishadam*. Dignity matters more here than in most placements. Where the Sun is genuinely strong, this native fights clean. Where it isn't, the wins still come, but the sorrow comes with them.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Sun in the 6th house good for defeating enemies?** Yes, this is one of the Sun's naturally strong houses: *Ripudhwamsakrid* names a consistent, repeatable capacity to overcome rivals.
**Does Sun in the 6th house cause financial loss?** The text names two specific drains, expenditure through government dealings and through friends, alongside risk to the maternal family.
**Does dignity of the Sun matter for this placement?** Yes, strength across the *Shadvarga* divisional charts sharpens the victories and eases the emotional cost named as *Vishadam*.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Surya Phalam, Verse 6, for the enemy-defeating capacity, expenditure through state and friends, maternal family risk, and travel distress. *Brihat Samhita*, Chapter 3, for the Sun's nature as *Aditya-Chara*. *Brihat Jataka*, for the Sun-Moon relationship and the *Shadvarga* strength framework (Stanza 6).
