**Quick answer:** Sun in the 12th house (Vyaya Bhava, the house of loss and dissolution) makes the native a "victorious sufferer": real triumph in direct conflict with rivals, alongside eye trouble, recurring bodily pain, status that is gained only to dissolve, financial loss while traveling, and risk to the paternal uncle.

## What Sun in the 12th House Means for Health
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Surya Phalam, Verse 12), read within the broader tradition of Varahamihira and Mantreswara that frames the 12th as *Vyaya Bhava*, the house of expenditure, loss, and dissolution. *Netra-dosha*, eye trouble, is specific rather than general: since the 2nd house governs the right eye, the 12th governs the left, and the Sun, the significator of sight itself, targets that eye directly from this position. Alongside it, *Deha-duhkham*: the Sun's heat here does not translate into vitality, it shows up as recurring bodily pain instead. A house built around loss does not make an exception for the body that carries the native through it.

## Sun in the 12th House: Conflict and Victory
*Vipaksha-ahave Jayashrih* is the placement's one clean gift, and it is worth naming clearly: genuine victory and glory in direct confrontation with rivals. The Sun's *Tejas* is not destroyed by the 12th house, it is redirected, away from the native's own health and toward crushing whatever stands opposed to them in open contest. **The native wins the fight. The win does not come free**, as the rest of this placement makes clear.

## Sun in the 12th House: Career and Status
*Sthitirlabdhayā liyate* is the placement's throughline: a position, once attained, dissolves. Status is genuinely earned here and then genuinely lost, not through one dramatic reversal but as the house's basic pattern.

## Sun in the 12th House: Travel and Family
*Hanir-adhva-pradeshe* names financial loss specifically while in transit or on the road, consistent with the 12th house's long association with travel and foreign lands. Alongside it sits a named risk to the paternal uncle, *Pitrvya-apado*, calamity or danger to that specific relative. Gains here are real, but they do not stay put, and safety does not automatically extend to everyone the native is connected to.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Sun in the 12th house good or bad?** Both, genuinely: real victory in direct conflict, alongside real loss to health, status, and stability, described in the text as a "victorious sufferer."
**Does Sun in the 12th house affect the eyes?** Yes, specifically the left eye (*Netra-dosha*), since the 12th house governs the left eye and the 2nd governs the right.
**Does Sun in the 12th house cause financial loss?** Yes, in two specific ways: status attained and then dissolved (*Sthitirlabdhayā liyate*), and loss while traveling (*Hanir-adhva-pradeshe*).

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Surya Phalam, Verse 12, for every claim above: eye and body affliction (*Netra-dosha*, *Deha-duhkham*), victory in conflict (*Vipaksha-ahave Jayashrih*), status gained and dissolved (*Sthitirlabdhayā liyate*), travel loss (*Hanir-adhva-pradeshe*), and danger to the paternal uncle (*Pitrvya-apado*).
