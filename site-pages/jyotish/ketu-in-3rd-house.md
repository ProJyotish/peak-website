**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 3rd house (Vikrama Bhava, the house of courage and siblings) produces a commanding outer presence paired with real internal agitation, destroys enemies while generating wealth and disputes in the process, thins out the circle of friends and siblings, and creates chronic arm pain. No sign-based exception is given.

## Ketu in the 3rd House: Personality
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Ketu Verse 3), read alongside the *Brihat Jataka*'s foundational definitions of the third house. Classically the *Vikrama Bhava*, the third house governs courage and martial prowess (*vikrama*), siblings (*sahaja*, *sodara*), and mental firmness (*dhairya*). Ketu here, called *Shikhi*, brings its usual capacity for *Udvega* (agitation) and for gains won through unconventional, disruptive means. The text draws a sharp line between appearance and interior state: outwardly there is *Tejo-adhikam*, splendor in excess, an authoritative presence that reads as more formidable than the native's actual standing. Inwardly sit *Bhaya-udvega* and *Chinta-akulatvam*, fearful agitation and anxiety-driven turmoil.

## Ketu in the 3rd House: Wealth, Enemies, and Disputes
This is one of the stronger Ketu placements materially. The text credits *Shatru-nasham*, the outright destruction of enemies, and *Dhanam Bhogam Aishvarya*: wealth, luxury, and personal grandeur. The mechanism is the native's own sharpness rather than favor granted from above, and the cost is named directly: *Vivadam*, disputes. Dismantling the competition tends to generate more competition to dismantle.

## Ketu in the 3rd House: Siblings and Friends
The relational signature is separative. *Suhrid-varga-nasham* is the loss or thinning of the native's circle of friends and relatives. *Sodore-ghata* (or *nasham*) applies the same erosion specifically to siblings: trouble, or outright loss of the support that brothers and sisters are supposed to provide.

## Ketu in the 3rd House: Health Effects
The *Brihat Jataka* maps the third sign to the *Hridaya* (breast), and the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* adds *Sada Bahu-pidam*, chronic, recurring pain in the arms. The word *sada*, "always," marks this as a standing condition rather than an occasional strain.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Ketu in the 3rd house good for career?** Materially yes, it destroys enemies and brings wealth, but through dispute-generating means rather than smooth advancement.
**Does Ketu in the 3rd house affect siblings?** Yes, the text names trouble or loss regarding siblings directly (*Sodore-ghata*).
**What health issues come with Ketu in the 3rd house?** Chronic arm pain (*Sada Bahu-pidam*) is named specifically in the *Chamatkara Chintaamani*.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Ketu Verse 3. *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verse 4: anatomical mapping of the third house to the breast (*Hridaya*/*Uras*).
