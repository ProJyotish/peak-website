**Quick answer:** Sun in the 7th house (Saptamabhava, the house of partnership) is one of the Sun's least forgiving placements: real heat and hardship directed at the spouse, persistent worry over the relationship, physical pain and insomnia tied directly to a competitive mindset, and thin returns in trade despite real effort.

## What Sun in the 7th House Means for Marriage
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintamani* (Surya Phalam, Verse 7), sitting within the broader tradition of *Brihat Samhita*, *Brihat Jataka*, and Mantreswara's *Phaladeepika* for how *Saptamabhava*, the 7th house, the seat of partnership and open dealing, is read generally. *Priya-tapanam* is direct: the Sun's heat, here in the house of the partner, afflicts the spouse specifically. The word is "heating," not a euphemism, real hardship for the person the native is bound to. Alongside it sits *Chinta*, and this is worth being precise about: it's not free-floating anxiety. The text ties it to the marriage itself, persistent worry over the partner's wellbeing and the stability of the domestic sphere.

## Sun in the 7th House: Body and Sleep
*Pida-pida*, recurring physical pain, shows up alongside a mind that won't rest. The text draws a direct line, not a loose association, between the native's insomnia (*na eti nidram*, sleep never comes) and a specifically competitive environment (*pratispardhaya*). This is a native whose mind stays occupied by rivalry and professional friction late enough into the night that rest doesn't happen. It is a specific mechanism, not a general anxiety: this native isn't losing sleep over everything, they're losing it over losing.

## Sun in the 7th House: Trade and Career
*Tuccha-labdhih kraye-vikraye*: buying and selling, direct commercial activity, yields only meager returns. Paired with the competitive pressure named above, the picture holds together: this native can work hard in trade and still see thin margins, because attention and energy are being spent on rivalry rather than on the deal itself. The irony is exact. The same intensity that makes this native formidable in a rivalry is what keeps the returns thin when the actual goal is just a fair trade.

## Where it gets complicated
There isn't a separate complication to carve out here. The whole placement is the complication, and it repeats the same shape three times over: the spouse suffers from the native's heat, the native's own mind won't stop competing long enough to rest, and commerce, pursued with real effort, comes back with thin results. Partnership, the 7th house's core territory, is where this Sun does the most damage. **The texts are consistent here, and they don't soften any of it.**

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Sun in the 7th house bad for marriage?** The classical text is direct about it: *Priya-tapanam* names real hardship for the spouse, and *Chinta* names ongoing worry over the relationship.
**Why does Sun in the 7th house cause insomnia?** The text ties sleeplessness (*na eti nidram*) directly to a competitive mindset (*pratispardhaya*), a mind too occupied with rivalry to rest.
**Is Sun in the 7th house good for business?** Not for direct trade specifically: the text calls the returns from buying and selling *tuccha-labdhih*, meager, even with real effort.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Surya Phalam, Verse 7, for every specific claim: spousal affliction, anxiety, physical pain, insomnia tied to competition, and meager trade results. *Brihat Samhita*, *Brihat Jataka*, and *Phaladeepika* provide the general framework for 7th-house interpretation this verse sits within.
