**Quick answer:** Mercury in the 7th house (Saptama Bhava or Kalatra Bhava, the house of marriage) gives a peaceful marriage built on conversation and mental compatibility with an intelligent, youthful spouse, a body with a golden luster, and real commercial and medical aptitude. The catch is physical: Mercury is *Napunsaka*, neuter, and lacks *Digbala* here, which shows up as low vitality and a ceiling on accumulating great wealth.

## Mercury in the 7th House: Marriage and Relationships
*Chamatkara Chintamani* is specific: *"सुतः शीतगोः सप्तमे शं युवत्या विधत्ते,"* Mercury in the seventh brings peace and comfort with a young wife. The spouse is intelligent, fair-complexioned, and youthful in bearing, and the marriage runs on verbal exchange and mental compatibility rather than heavy emotional weather. *Sham*, tranquility, holds unless *Krura-grahas*, cruel planets, get involved.

## Mercury in the 7th House: Physical Vitality and Health
The text gives the native *"हेमवद् देहशोभां,"* a body with the luster of gold, pristine and well-proportioned enough to command respect in public. Then it takes something back: the same verse names *"तुच्छ वीर्यं च भोगे,"* meager potency, low vitality in physical enjoyment, a direct consequence of a neuter planet occupying the house that corresponds to the genital organ (*Brihat Jataka*, 1.3). Mercury sits 180 degrees from the Lagna here, meaning it has no *Digbala*, which it only achieves in the 1st house, and that missing directional strength shows up as a real deficit in raw physical stamina, with a predisposition toward sensitivity in the urinary and reproductive tracts.

## Mercury in the 7th House: Career, Wealth, and Social Standing
As a natural *Vanijya* significator sitting in a *Kendra*, the native has real commercial and medical (*Chikitsa*) aptitude, sharp negotiation, and standing as a mediator or diplomat. *Chamatkara Chintamani* is blunt about the limit: *"न शक्रोति सत्सम्पदो वानुकर्तुम्,"* unable to truly acquire or emulate great wealth. Mercury lacks the expansive weight of Brihaspati or the aggressive acquisitive drive of Mangal, so prosperity stays intellectual and aesthetic rather than turning into massive material hoards. *Phaladeepika* (Chapter 8, Verse 9) confirms the *Kendra* strength: the native is learned, well-presented, and marries someone of similar nature.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mercury in the 7th house good for marriage?** Yes, it favours a peaceful, communication-driven marriage with an intelligent, youthful spouse.
**Does Mercury in the 7th house affect physical vitality?** Yes, the texts name reduced vitality directly, tied to Mercury's neuter nature and its lack of directional strength in this house.
**Does Mercury in the 7th house bring great wealth?** No. It supports intellectual and commercial success, but the classical text names a real ceiling on accumulating major wealth.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintamani*, Budha, Verse 7 (marriage, golden body, low vitality, wealth ceiling); *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1, Verse 3 (7th house as genital organ) and Chapter 2 (Budha as *Napunsaka*); *Phaladeepika*, Chapter 8, Verse 9 (*Kendra* strength).
