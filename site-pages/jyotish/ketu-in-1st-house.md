**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 1st house (Tanu Bhava, the house of the body and self) gives a temperament that cannot settle, chronic Vata-driven bodily pain, friction with kinsmen, a wary fear of malicious people, and a marriage clouded by anxiety that originates inside the native rather than in the relationship itself. No sign-based exception is offered for this placement.

## Ketu in the 1st House: Personality and Temperament
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* of Bhattanarayana (Ketu Section, Verse 1). Ketu here is called *Shikhi*, the crested one, classically described as headless. The 1st house is *Tanu Bhava*, governing the head and the *Manas* (mind), and a headless force placed there produces exactly what the anatomy suggests: a vacuum of focus. The text names two distinct states. *Vyakulatvam* is a baseline lack of *sthirata* (steadiness), the native feeling chronically uprooted. *Sadodvegata* is sharper, a constant excitement or apprehension, a trembling that is closer to a resting state than an occasional mood.

## Ketu in the 1st House: Health Effects
The text is specific about where the instability lands physically: *Maruti*, wind-related or *Vata* disorders. Ketu is airy, dry, and erratic by classical description, and placed in the house of the body (*deha*) it produces joint pain, digestive gas, and, in the text's own words, *sharire vyatha naikadha maruti syat*, multiple kinds of bodily pain from wind imbalance.

## Ketu in the 1st House: Family and Social Life
Two separate signatures appear here. *Bandhava-kleshakarta*: the native becomes a source of distress to his own kinsmen, his restlessness landing on brothers and close relatives as a burden. And *durjanebhyo bhayam*: a fear of wicked people, a heightened vulnerability to the malice of others that produces a cautious, defensive posture toward the world over time.

## Ketu in the 1st House: Marriage
The instability does not stay contained to the self. The *Chamatkara Chintaamani* extends it to the spouse through *Kalatradi-chinta*, a persistent, deep-seated preoccupation with the partner's welfare and the marriage's stability. The mechanism matters: Ketu in the Lagna disturbs the native's own peace first, and that agitation gets projected onto the domestic sphere. Even a stable marriage can be experienced as under threat, because the threat originates inside the native.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Ketu in the 1st house good or bad?** Difficult by classical standard: the verse gives no reversal clause, and names familial discord, health trouble, and marital anxiety as standing conditions rather than risks.
**Does Ketu in the 1st house affect health?** Yes, specifically through Vata (wind) imbalance, described as chronic bodily pain.
**Does Ketu in the 1st house affect marriage?** Yes, the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* names ongoing preoccupation and anxiety about the spouse directly.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Ketu Section, Verse 1. *Brihat Jataka*, Chapter 1: definition of the Tanu Bhava and planetary lordship. *Brihat Samhita*, Chapter 11: Ketu as comet (Dhumketu), contextual only, not house-based natal results.
