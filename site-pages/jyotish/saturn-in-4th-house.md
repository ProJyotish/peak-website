**Quick answer:** Saturn in the 4th house (*Chaturtha Bhava*, the seat of domestic security) unroots the native from the ancestral home and its wealth, and makes the native a source of grief to both parents rather than the reverse. Property and vehicles are prone to loss or decline, and health tends toward *Vata*, wind-based or neurological trouble, often felt in the chest.

## Distance From the Ancestral Estate

The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Shani-Phalam, Verse 4): "चतुर्थे शनौ पैकृतं याति दूरं धनं मन्दिरं बन्धुवर्गापवादः । पितुश्चापि मातुश्च सन्तापकारी गृहे वाहने हानयो वातरोगी ॥ ४ ॥" The 4th house is the "heart" of the *Kalapurusha* in the *Brihat Jataka*'s mapping, which is precisely the register this verse operates in. *Paikritam-yati-duram*: the native goes far from paternal property, a life lived away from the ancestral home rather than rooted in it. The verse also names *Dhana-mandiram*, wealth tied to the family mansion or estate, as something the native stays distant from. This is not a placement about losing the family home in a dispute. It is about never quite being anchored to it in the first place.

## Grief the Native Causes, Not Just Absorbs

*Pitu-cha-api-matu-cha-santapakari* is direct: the native becomes a source of mental grief and distress to both mother and father. This is not a native afflicted by difficult parents. It is a native whose own presence and choices generate the distress. That friction does not stay private, either: *Bandhu-varga-apavada*, reproach and censure from relatives, tends to spill into public scandal, and the native often ends up cast as *Vivadi*, a disputant defending his own character against that reproach.

## Property, Vehicles, and Health

*Hanayo* covers loss, damage, or decline in vehicles and the house itself, striking directly at the 4th house's role as the significator of fixed assets. On health, the native is prone to *Vata-rogi*, wind-based or neurological disease. Since Saturn occupies the "heart" region of the *Kalapurusha* here, this affliction often shows up as chest-related trouble, feeding back into a guarded, closed-off mental posture that develops directly out of the friction at home.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Saturn in the 4th house mean loss of property?** The text points to loss, damage, or decline specifically in vehicles and the house (*hanayo*), and to distance from the ancestral estate rather than a single dramatic loss.

**Does Saturn in the 4th house affect the mother and father?** Yes, and specifically: the native is named as the source of the parents' distress, not the other way around.

**Is Saturn in the 4th house bad for health?** It leans toward *Vata*, wind-based or neurological trouble, often felt in the chest, per the classical mapping of this house to the heart of the *Kalapurusha*.

## Sources

*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Shani-Phalam, Verse 4, for the core results on parents, property, vehicles, and health. *Brihat Jataka*, for the 4th house's identity as the chest and heart of the *Kalapurusha*.
