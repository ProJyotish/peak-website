**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 10th house (Karmasthana, the house of career and status) turns worldly success into a burden, weakens support from the father, and afflicts the knees through vehicle-related risk. In four specific signs, checked against the Shadvarga divisional charts, the same placement instead destroys professional rivals outright.

## Ketu in the 10th House: Career
The governing verse is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Bhatta Narayana, Ketu Bhava, Verse 10): *"piturno sukhī karmagaḥ yasya ketuḥ durbhagaṃ kaṣṭabhāje karoti | tadā vāhane pīḍitaṃ jātu janma vṛṣājālikanyāsu cet śatrunāśam."* The tenth house, *Karmasthana* (also *Meshoorana*, a Sanskritized rendering of the Greek *mesouranēma*, mid-heaven), is the chart's highest point. Ketu here, *Shikhi*, is headless (*Kabandha*), bringing detachment and abrupt termination into the house that is supposed to reward sustained, visible effort. The verse's central term is *Kashtabhaje*, "recipient of hardship": worldly success itself gets experienced as a burden, felt as oppressive even when objectively productive. Alongside this sits *Durbhagam*, "unfortunate," a propensity for public reputation to take sudden setbacks.

## Ketu in the 10th House: Father
*Pitur-no sukhi*, "not happy from father," points to a structural lack of paternal support, whether through the father's own misfortune or a deeper disharmony in the paternal line. The practical result is a kind of karmic separation: the native tends to end up building status independent of whatever the family name might have provided.

## Ketu in the 10th House in Aries (Aja), Taurus (Vrisha), Scorpio (Ali), or Virgo (Kanya)
When Ketu occupies one of these four signs in the tenth house, the result is *Shatrunasham*, the absolute destruction of enemies: an indomitable edge over professional rivals, regardless of how much *Kashta* the process still involves. Outside these four signs, this particular relief is not available. Even inside them, the *Brihat Jataka*'s *Shadvarga* framework, the six divisional charts, makes clear that whether *Shatrunasham* actually manifests depends on Ketu's strength across those divisions, with the *Navamsa* lord's condition singled out as the primary determinant.

## Ketu in the 10th House: Health Effects
*Vahane piditam*, suffering through vehicles, points to elevated risk around transport accidents. The *Brihat Jataka* maps the tenth house to the knees (*Janu*) of the *Kalapurusha*, and combined with Ketu's tendency toward sudden, tail-like injury, the resulting hardship plausibly concentrates as chronic joint pain or sudden injury to the knees and lower legs.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does Ketu in the 10th house harm career?** It complicates rather than blocks it: success is described as a burden (*Kashtabhaje*), felt as oppressive even when productive.
**Which signs let Ketu in the 10th house destroy enemies?** Aries, Taurus, Scorpio, and Virgo, per the *Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Verse 10, subject to the strength of the *Shadvarga* divisions.
**Does Ketu in the 10th house affect the relationship with the father?** Yes, *Pitur-no sukhi* states directly that the native does not receive happiness from the father.

## Sources
