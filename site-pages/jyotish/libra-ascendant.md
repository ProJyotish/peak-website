**Quick answer:** The Libra ascendant is ruled by Venus and represented by a figure holding scales in a marketplace, governing the abdomen and genital organ in the *Kalapurusha*. Unlike most lagnas, Libra is unusually sensitive to whichever planet joins it in the first house, so much so that the classical texts describe a different body and a different temperament for nearly every possible occupant.

## Fundamental Identity: Venus, the Scales, and the Marketplace

Tula (Libra), the seventh sign, governs the abdomen and the genital organ in the *Kalapurusha* (*Brihat Jataka*, 1.4) and is depicted as a person standing in a marketplace holding a pair of scales, weighing options and maintaining balance (1.4). Venus (*Sukra*) rules it (1.6), supplying the drive for beauty and relational harmony, always filtered through an analytical lens.

## What Each Planet in the Lagna Does

Tula's classical texts break the native's body and mind down planet by planet more explicitly than most signs:

- **Sun:** a tall body (*Tungayashti*), real suffering from wind and bile imbalance, and a restless, traveling nature.
- **Moon:** beauty and abundant joy (*Lavanya-manandapurnam*) when strong; when weak, a dull appearance and risk of hearing impairment.
- **Mercury:** a body "resembling divine gold" (*Chamikaribhuta*), often functioning as a healer, plus an eloquent voice.
- **Jupiter:** a divine, beautiful body, a preference for fine clothing, and a priestly aura, thinking turned spiritual and afterlife-focused (*Paraloki*).
- **Venus:** the lagna lord in its own sign, granting beautiful limbs and an affectionate, celebratory temperament.
- **Saturn:** thick or large eyes (*Sthuladrishti*), and a temperament tilting toward jealousy and suspicion (*Matsara*).
- **Rahu:** an eloquent voice and the capacity to destroy an enemy's strength and influence, at the cost of a lack of steadiness (*adhriti*) in marriage.

## Personality and Mental Peace

Mental peace splits cleanly by occupant. Sun or Mars in the lagna brings *Manah Santape*, real distress; Moon or Jupiter brings a joyful, peaceful mind. There is no single "Tula temperament" apart from what actually sits in the first house.

## Health Tendencies

Wind and bile disorders run chronic, especially with the Sun or Ketu present. Mars in the lagna brings recurring head and eye pain. A weak Moon carries real risk to hearing.

## Special Yogas for Libra Ascendant (Bhavartha Ratnakara)

- **Wealth yoga:** Moon and Venus in Libra indicate significant wealth and prosperity, particularly during the Venus *dasa* (Points 12-13).
- **Fortunate dasa:** Mars and Venus in the lagna with Jupiter in Capricorn make both the Mercury and Jupiter *dasas* highly fortunate (Point 10).
- **Sacred dips:** Saturn, Mercury, and Mars in Capricorn with Rahu in Pisces bring dips in holy rivers such as the Ganga, during the *dasas* of Mars and Rahu (Point 11).
- **The lunar affliction:** the Moon in the first house is not very auspicious here and afflicts other *dhana yogas* present in the chart; the native becomes truly fortunate with the Moon in the lagna only if the ascendant is a sign other than Taurus or Tula (Points 14-15).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the typical personality traits of a Libra ascendant?**
A Libra ascendant weighs options and seeks balance, ruled by Venus, though the specific temperament depends heavily on which planet occupies the first house.

**Is the Moon a good placement for a Libra ascendant?**
No. The Moon in the first house is specifically excluded from the general auspiciousness rule for Libra, along with Taurus, and tends to afflict wealth combinations instead.

**Which dasha brings wealth for a Libra ascendant?**
The Venus *dasa* is named specifically, especially when Moon and Venus are placed together in Libra, and the Mercury and Jupiter *dasas* are also fortunate under the right Mars-Venus-Jupiter configuration.
