**Quick answer:** Sun in the 2nd house (Dhana Bhava, the house of wealth and family) gives fortune that is providential rather than earned by direct hustle, wealth channeled toward good causes, commanding speech and presence, and reliable friction at home, specifically with the spouse. Direct profit-seeking tends to backfire; wealth that arrives as a byproduct of right conduct holds.

## What Sun in the 2nd House Means for Wealth
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* of Bhatta Narayana (Surya Phalam, Verse 2), read against the *Brihat Jataka*'s architecture of *Dhana Bhava*, the second house. *Dhana Bhava* maps to the face (*Mukha*) of the *Kalapurusha*, per *Brihat Jataka* 1.4, and governs wealth, family, speech, and what sustains the body. The *Brihat Jataka* also gives the principle *Surja rasmischandramah*: the Moon's light is only the Sun's reflection. Since the Moon ordinarily governs the fluid, everyday texture of family and sustenance, putting the Sun where the Moon normally works means both identity and family get scorched by something brighter and harder than they're built for.

The verse calls the native *bhagya-adhika*, exceptionally fortunate, with happiness through *catuspat*, historically four-legged wealth such as livestock and conveyances: the kind of asset that builds steady value rather than a windfall. Money that arrives tends toward *sad-vyaye*, righteous expenditure, channeled toward good causes rather than hoarded.

## Sun in the 2nd House: Family and Marriage
**Domestic peace is where this placement spends its cost.** *Kutumbe kalih jayaya jayate*: strife in the household, and the verse names the source specifically, the spouse (*Jaya*), not a vague "family tension." The Sun's authority, useful everywhere else, runs too bright for the give-and-take a household actually needs.

## Sun in the 2nd House: Career and Money-Making
The text names a real paradox in professional life: *kriya nishphala labhasya hetoh*, action undertaken specifically to generate *labha*, mercantile profit, is fruitless. Wealth here is providential, not mercantile. Treat it as a target to be pursued directly and it resists. Let it accumulate as a byproduct of right conduct and *sad-vyaye* spending, and it holds. The frustration of direct profit-seeking is not just financial: the text ties it to *Tapa*, the Sun's internal heat, which under repeated failure becomes mental exhaustion.

## Sun in the 2nd House: Speech, Face, and Health
Because *Dhana Bhava* governs the face and mouth (*Mukha*), and the *Brihat Samhita*'s Chapter 51 *Anga-vidya* tradition assigns this house the same body parts, the Sun's presence here shows up physically as well as socially. Speech and bearing carry a commanding, authoritative charge, a presence people register in a room, and that authority reads in the eyes and features too. Being a *Krura* (harsh) planet, the Sun's heat here also brings a tendency toward eye and heat-related sensitivity.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Sun in the 2nd house good for wealth?** Yes, the native is called *bhagya-adhika*, exceptionally fortunate, but the fortune is providential, not the result of chasing profit directly.
**Does Sun in the 2nd house cause marriage problems?** The text names domestic strife specifically through the spouse (*Jaya*), so yes, this placement carries a real cost at home.
**Why does direct profit-seeking fail with Sun in the 2nd house?** The verse calls actions taken purely for *labha* (mercantile gain) *nishphala*, fruitless, because this Sun delivers wealth as a byproduct of right conduct, not as a reward for hustle.

## Sources
