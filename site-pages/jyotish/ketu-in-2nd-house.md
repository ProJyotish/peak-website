**Quick answer:** Ketu in the 2nd house (Dhana Bhava, the house of wealth and family) scatters accumulated wealth and food security, creates opposition within the family, pairs oddly with speech that is publicly respected, brings restlessness about standing with authority, and predisposes the native to mouth and facial ailments. In its own sign or a benefic's sign, the entire picture reverses into "great happiness."

## Ketu in the 2nd House: Wealth and Material Loss
The governing verse is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Ketu Bhava Phala, Verse 2). The second house is *Dhana Bhava* (wealth) and *Kutumba Bhava* (family) together, and Ketu here, called *Shikhi* and separately *Rahu-puccha* ("the Dragon's Tail"), is a *Chaya Graha* (shadow planet) whose default mode is detachment. The verse's operative phrase is *dhana-dhanya-nasha*: a systematic depletion of resources and sustenance, split into *dhana-nasha* (loss of liquid capital, jewelry, inherited assets) and *dhanya-nasha* (loss of food grains and basic material security). Retaining wealth through ordinary means is unusually hard under this placement.

## Ketu in the 2nd House: Family Opposition and Speech
*Kutumbad virodha*, family opposition, names a persistent lack of alignment with kin, producing psychological or physical distance from the family unit. Set against this is a genuine paradox: the text credits *vachah satkritam*, speech that is honored and respected, likely because Ketu's detachment produces a cutting, unvarnished clarity that lands well outside the home even as it costs goodwill inside it. The verse adds *avyagrata kim nareshat*, a specific restlessness about standing with authority or the state, an underlying insecurity about rank that persists even when the outward position looks secure.

## Ketu in the 2nd House: Health Effects
The second house governs the face and mouth, and the verse is direct: *mukhe-roga-krit*, a predisposition to disease or lesions localized to the mouth and face.

## Ketu in the 2nd House in Own or Benefic Sign
The verse supplies its own reversal clause: *sve-grihe saumya-gehe-ati-saukhyam*. When Ketu sits in its own sign or in a benefic's sign, the entire picture inverts to *ati-saukhyam*, "great happiness," with domestic stability and the preservation, rather than the scattering, of assets.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Does Ketu in the 2nd house cause poverty?** Not poverty exactly; the text describes poor retention of wealth and grain rather than outright destitution, unless dignity is present.
**Does Ketu in the 2nd house affect family relationships?** Yes, the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* names family opposition (*kutumbad virodha*) directly.
**Is there a remedy or reversal for Ketu in the 2nd house?** Yes, the verse itself states that Ketu in its own sign or a benefic's sign produces *ati-saukhyam*, great happiness, instead.
