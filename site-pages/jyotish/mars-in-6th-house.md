**Quick answer:** Mars in the 6th house (Shatru Bhava, the house of enemies, debt, and disease) sits inside its own kind of native territory. The text describes decisive victory over enemies, a wise and content temperament, and wealth that dissolves before it returns in abundance. The one clear cost named is to the maternal uncle, whose fortune does not mirror the native's own.

## What Mars in the 6th House Means for Enemies and Conflict
The primary source is the *Chamatkara Chintaamani* (Bhauma, Verse 6), read alongside the general framework of the *Bhavartha Ratnakara*. The verse states it almost as a law: *na tishthanti shashthe-arayo*, enemies do not stand in the 6th house. Not that they're absent, but that they cannot maintain their position against this native. The text specifies dominance in *Sangara*, battle, meaning these are decisive wins, not quiet diplomatic ones.

## Mars in the 6th House: Personality and Wisdom
The scholarly surprise in this verse is what it does to temperament. Mars is naturally heat and impulse, but the text describes the native here as *Manishi*, a wise or learned person, and *Sukhi*, happy. The native doesn't just fight, he comes to understand the mechanics of conflict well enough that it reads as wisdom.

## Mars in the 6th House: Wealth and Career
The financial formula in the verse is precise: *viliyet vittam labhettapi bhuri*. Wealth first dissolves, *viliyet*, typically during periods of active conflict or debt this house forces on the native, and only then returns in *bhuri*, abundance. This is the 6th house's *Upachaya* (growth-through-struggle) character working as designed: the loss is a stated prerequisite, not a warning sign.

## Mars in the 6th House: Health Effects
The 6th house is *Roga Bhava*, the house of disease, and the *Bhavartha Ratnakara*'s chapter structure confirms this is the seat for disease analysis, but the specific verse-level results for Mars here have not survived in the available source fragments. The general *Phaladeepika* (Chapter 16) principle holds that malefics in *Upachaya* houses gain strength to overcome physical setbacks, which is a reasonable inference here, not a citation specific to this exact placement.

## Mars in the 6th House: Effects on Maternal Uncle
Stated flatly in the text: the native's rise does not extend to the maternal uncle. *Matuleyo na tadvan*, regarding the maternal uncle, "not like that." The uncle's fortune does not mirror the native's own.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Is Mars in the 6th house good or bad?** Largely good for the native's own position: decisive wins over enemies and eventual wealth, though the maternal uncle does not share in that fortune.
**Does Mars in the 6th house affect health?** The house governs disease generally, but no verse-specific Mars result survives in the source material; only a general principle about malefic strength in this type of house applies.
**Does Mars in the 6th house bring wealth or losses?** Both, in sequence: wealth dissolves first, then returns in abundance.

## Sources
*Chamatkara Chintaamani*, Bhauma, Verse 6. *Phaladeepika*, Chapter 16 (general principle, not verse-specific). *Bhavartha Ratnakara* (structural reference for disease analysis, no surviving specific verse for this placement).
